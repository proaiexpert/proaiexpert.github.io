ALINA HORB · BILINGUAL WEBSITE CASE

LIVE · UA/RU · IN REFINEMENT

A bilingual website designed to build trust before the first consultation.

Website strategy, personal brand, localized content and production design for psychologist Alina Horb.

ProAI Expert connected personal presence, professional boundaries, expert content and first contact in one coherent digital experience.

Explore the case View live website (external website)

18 routes · 2 languages · 4 expert topics · 8 localized articles

THE BRIEF

A specialist website should answer questions before the first message.

Alina’s website needed to introduce a real person, explain a sensitive professional service and make the first step understandable without reducing the practice to a biography and a booking button.

The task was to build a complete website around a personal practice—not decorate a profile.

THE TEMPLATE RISK

Trust disappears when every specialist looks interchangeable.

Generic wellness imagery, anonymous service cards and stock-style calmness can make a psychologist look polished while removing everything distinctive.

The website needed to replace excess credentials and generic booking flows with Alina’s presence, language, working principles and clear professional boundaries.

TRUST STRATEGY

Clarity comes before commitment.

Before contact details are requested, the website explains confidentiality, professional boundaries, crisis limitations and the visitor’s right to stop.

Trust is built into the route: essential conditions are explained before the form, not hidden after it.

PERSONAL BRAND

A personal brand that keeps the person at the centre.

Portrait, typography, space and restrained colour create recognition without clinical coldness or generic wellness styling.

UA / RU SEARCH ARCHITECTURE

Two languages. Two ways to be found.

Separate URLs, natural-language content, localized metadata and mapped article routes allow Ukrainian and Russian visitors to enter the website directly in the context they searched in.

Browser translation can help someone read an open page, but it does not replace a complete localized search architecture.

Separate URLs · Localized metadata · Self-canonical · Hreflang · X-default · Localized articles

CONSULTATION JOURNEY

The process becomes understandable before the message is sent.

  1. 01 · Short message

    A few sentences are enough to begin.

    The visitor does not need a diagnosis or a perfectly formulated request.

  2. 02 · Alina’s reply

    The first response clarifies the possible next step.

    The conversation can continue with more context and less uncertainty.

  3. 03 · Practical alignment

    Format, timing and organizational details are agreed.

    The first contact stays useful without becoming an assessment.

  4. 04 · First consultation

    The conversation begins with clearer expectations and context.

    Practical questions have already been addressed.

FIRST CONTACT

A shorter form for a sensitive first step.

Name, reply contact, a short message and consent are required. Communication channel, format, time zone and availability remain optional.

Required:
Name · Reply contact · Message · Consent

Optional:
Channel · Format · Time zone · Availability

The screenshot documents the first-contact structure. It does not claim successful form delivery.

EXPERT CONTENT

Four topics become eight localized article pages.

Notes is a structured publishing layer rather than a decorative blog. Four expert topics exist in Ukrainian and Russian and lead to complete long-form articles.

4 topics × 2 languages = 8 localized article pages

RESPONSIVE PRODUCTION

The website preserves its character on every screen.

The mobile experience recomposes portrait, headline and action rather than shrinking the desktop composition.

18 routes · 16 indexable content routes · 2 privacy routes · No-JS · Reduced motion

THE RESULT

From a personal practice to a complete bilingual website.

The finished system connects personal identity, professional boundaries, consultation guidance, expert publishing and first contact across Ukrainian and Russian routes.

For ProAI Expert, the project demonstrates how strategy, brand direction, content architecture and production can support a field where trust matters before conversion.

This case documents strategy, design, content architecture and production work. It does not claim clinical outcomes, rankings, traffic growth, enquiry growth, conversion or revenue.
