ALINA HORB · REAL WEBSITE PROJECT · LIVE · IN REFINEMENT

A personal practice, shaped into a bilingual digital sanctuary.

Strategy, personal brand, editorial design and production architecture for a psychologist’s Ukrainian–Russian website.

ProAI Expert connected personal presence, professional boundaries, localized content and first contact in one calm digital product.

01 · THE BRIEF

A personal practice needed more than an online introduction.

Alina’s website needed to introduce a real person, explain a sensitive professional service and make the first step understandable without reducing the practice to a biography and a booking button.

The task was to build a digital product around a personal practice—not decorate a profile.

02 · THE RISK

Trust disappears when every expert looks interchangeable.

Generic wellness imagery and familiar service cards can make a specialist look polished while removing everything distinctive. The project had to express Alina’s presence, language, working principles and professional boundaries.

No stock wellness language · No generic therapist template · No credential wall

03 · TRUST BEFORE ACTION

Clarity comes before commitment.

Before asking for contact details, the website explains how the work begins, what the consultation can and cannot provide, and where professional or crisis boundaries apply.

Confidentiality · Competence boundaries · Right to stop · Crisis limitation · Outcome orientation

04 · PERSONAL BRAND

A visual identity that keeps the person at its centre.

Premium Editorial Sanctuary uses portrait presence, ivory space, restrained colour and serif typography to create recognition without clinical coldness or sentimental wellness styling.

05 · UA / RU SEARCH ARCHITECTURE

Two languages. Two ways to be found.

The Ukrainian and Russian experiences are not two labels attached to one page. Each language has its own URLs, headings, natural-language copy, metadata, internal links and article routes, so a visitor can enter the website directly in the context they searched in.

Browser translation can help someone understand an open page, but it does not replace a localized search architecture. Separate self-canonical routes, reciprocal language signals and context-preserving navigation allow each language to function as an independent audience entry system.

The Ukrainian and Russian homepages share one visual system while remaining independent language-specific entry routes.

Separate URLs · Localized metadata · Self-canonical · Reciprocal hreflang · X-default · Context-preserving switch · Localized articles

This is a localized search foundation, not a claim of rankings, traffic growth or commercial SEO results.

06 · THE CONSULTATION JOURNEY

The process becomes understandable before the message is sent.

The route is explained in four calm steps: a short message, Alina’s reply, alignment of practical details and the first consultation. The visitor does not need a diagnosis or a perfectly formulated request.

  1. 01

    Short message

    A brief description is enough to begin.

  2. 02

    Alina’s reply

    The first response clarifies whether and how the conversation can continue.

  3. 03

    Detail alignment

    Format, timing and practical details are agreed without turning the first contact into an assessment.

  4. 04

    First consultation

    The meeting begins with context, pace and expectations already clearer.

07 · FIRST CONTACT

A shorter form for a sensitive first step.

Name, reply contact, a short message and consent are required. Communication channel, format, time zone and availability remain optional, keeping the first step compact while preserving useful context.

Required
name · reply contact · message · consent
Optional
channel · format · time zone · availability

Formspree delivery · Turnstile-enabled spam protection

08 · EXPERT CONTENT

Four topics become eight localized entry points.

Notes is a structured publishing layer rather than an occasional blog. The overview organizes four expert topics, while full Ukrainian and Russian article routes turn the system into a foundation for language-specific informational discovery.

4 topics × 2 languages = 8 localized article pages

The content system creates a search foundation. It does not prove current rankings or traffic.

09 · RESPONSIVE PRODUCTION

The editorial calm holds across screens, languages and routes.

The mobile experience recomposes portrait, headline and action instead of shrinking the desktop page. Behind that visual continuity are localized routes, metadata, structured content, accessible navigation and restrained progressive enhancement.

18 routes · 16 indexable content routes · 2 noindex privacy routes · Responsive navigation · No-JS completeness · Reduced motion

From a personal practice to a complete bilingual digital product.

The result connects personal identity, trust, consultation guidance, expert publishing and first contact in one Ukrainian–Russian system.
