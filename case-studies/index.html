PRODUCTION STANDARD

What stays consistent when the context changes.

Visual direction follows the business problem. The discipline behind the work stays explicit from definition through production.

DEFINE

Establish the decision system.

  • Strategy before surface
  • Information architecture
  • First meaningful action

PROVE

Keep evidence and claims aligned.

  • Truthful framing
  • Evidence discipline
  • Human-reviewed boundaries

PRODUCE

Recompose for real use.

  • Responsive re-composition
  • Localization where applicable
  • Accessibility and performance
  • Controlled motion
