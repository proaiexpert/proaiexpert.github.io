Overview

Project overview

ProAI Expert is a new AI automation and web studio requiring a premium bilingual website to establish its positioning and communicate two core service directions: AI Systems & Business Automation, and Websites & Digital Presence. The challenge was building a site that feels credible and premium without relying on visual gimmicks, while clearly separating and explaining both service areas to different types of prospective clients.

Scope

What was done

  • Full premium site direction: design system, palette, typography, and spacing
  • Homepage with clear hierarchy, two service directions, and editorial layout
  • AI Systems service page — systems-oriented, practical, and premium
  • Websites & Branding service page — lighter, more refined, more presentation-focused
  • Case Studies layer with index page and individual case templates
  • Full bilingual architecture with separate EN and RU page trees
Results

Key outcomes

Premium studio positioning clearly established
AI-first service direction credibly communicated
Two service directions presented with distinct character
Consistent premium feel across all EN and RU pages
