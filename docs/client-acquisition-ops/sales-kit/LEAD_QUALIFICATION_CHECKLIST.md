# ProAI Expert — Lead Qualification Checklist

**Status:** Approved for initial use  
**Prepared:** 2026-08-04  
**Operating owner:** Ihor Horb

## Purpose

Use this checklist before investing meaningful time in outreach, audits, discovery, or proposals.

## Step 1 — Target score

Score each criterion from 0 to 2.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Business activity | unclear/inactive | active | clearly active with customers/reviews |
| Problem severity | no visible problem | moderate friction | obvious trust/intake/response failure |
| Buyer access | no owner identified | indirect access | direct decision-maker reachable |
| Customer value | low/unknown | moderate | meaningful value per customer/job |
| Proof fit | weak | partial | strong match to case/demo |
| Local/language advantage | none | some | clear Washington or EN/RU/UA advantage |
| Buying signal | none | possible | hiring, expanding, advertising, rebranding, investing |
| Delivery fit | uncertain/custom | manageable | narrow and repeatable |

### Priority result

- `13–16` → **P1**: contact within 24 hours.
- `9–12` → **P2**: contact after P1; lighter personalization.
- `6–8` → **P3**: nurture, engage socially, or use partner route.
- `0–5` → **Disqualify from active selling**.

## Step 2 — Minimum record before first contact

Do not contact until these are recorded:

- business name;
- owner or decision-maker hypothesis;
- city and vertical;
- source;
- one truthful observed problem;
- lead score and priority;
- preferred contact route;
- likely relevant offer;
- next action and date.

## Step 3 — Audit eligibility

Create a personalized audit only when all are true:

- P1 or strong P2;
- problem is visible and material;
- owner accepted the audit, responded positively, or came through a strong introduction;
- a plausible paid next step exists;
- audit work will not displace delivery or higher-priority sales work.

If any condition is missing, send a short observation first.

## Step 4 — Qualified opportunity gate

A lead becomes a `Qualified Opportunity` only when every condition below is confirmed:

- [ ] Prospect acknowledges a specific problem.
- [ ] Decision-maker is involved or directly accessible.
- [ ] A credible reason to act exists within 90 days.
- [ ] A budget path exists for the responsible scope.
- [ ] ProAI Expert can deliver without uncontrolled custom work.

If one condition is missing, keep the lead in Engaged, Nurture, or Disqualified. Do not send a proposal.

## Step 5 — Discovery qualification questions

- Where do new customers come from now?
- Which customer or job is most valuable?
- What happens after a call, form, booking, or message?
- Where do inquiries become delayed, incomplete, or lost?
- What do prospects repeatedly misunderstand?
- What has already been tried?
- Why address the issue now?
- Who approves the project?
- Is there a budget path?
- What operational result would make the project worthwhile?

## Step 6 — Recommended outcome

Choose exactly one:

1. `Entry Sprint`
2. `Core Project`
3. `Nurture`
4. `Disqualify`

Do not present the full service catalogue.

## Disqualification triggers

Disqualify or nurture when:

- no active business or credible demand;
- only free strategy is wanted;
- budget is below minimum viable scope;
- guaranteed leads, revenue, rankings, legal, or compliance outcomes are demanded;
- extensive speculative work is requested;
- decision-maker remains inaccessible;
- meetings or information requests are repeatedly ignored;
- required capability or regulatory assurance is unavailable;
- payment, behavior, or integrity risk is unacceptable;
- buyer selects only by lowest price and rejects scope comparison.

## Proposal authorization

A proposal may be prepared only when:

- [ ] Qualified Opportunity gate is complete.
- [ ] Recommended offer is selected.
- [ ] Scope boundaries are clear.
- [ ] Client responsibilities are understood.
- [ ] Price and payment structure are internally approved.
- [ ] Decision date is agreed.
- [ ] Proposal follow-up is already scheduled in CRM.

## Final record requirement

Before ending any lead review, record:

- current stage;
- qualification outcome;
- one next action;
- next-action owner;
- next-action date.
