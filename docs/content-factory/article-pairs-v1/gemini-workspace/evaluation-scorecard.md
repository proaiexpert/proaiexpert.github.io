# Baseline vs Gemini Candidate — Evaluation Scorecard

Use this scorecard after Gemini Stage 2.

The baseline remains authoritative until a candidate is explicitly approved.

## Scoring

Score each dimension from 1 to 5.

- 1 — materially worse;
- 2 — weaker;
- 3 — equivalent or mixed;
- 4 — clearly better;
- 5 — exceptional improvement.

A high total score cannot override a factual, legal, evidence, or intent failure.

## Hard gates

A candidate automatically fails if it:

- introduces an unsupported claim;
- misstates an official source;
- materially changes primary intent;
- creates serious cannibalization;
- turns RU and EN into mechanical translations;
- implies legal advice or guaranteed results;
- uses an unapproved case claim;
- cannot be implemented accessibly on mobile.

## Final pair gate

Before implementation, confirm:

- facts aligned;
- metadata finalized;
- sources attached to claims;
- RU and EN independently written;
- hreflang/canonical plan correct;
- mobile table behavior resolved;
- CTA destination approved;
- no unapproved evidence link;
- checkpoint remains unchanged.
