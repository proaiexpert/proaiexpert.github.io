PASS.

> What level of language coverage can the business justify through real demand, operational capacity, and maintainable content governance?

Shared conclusions:

- owner language does not automatically determine public-site language;
- community demographics alone do not prove website demand;
- English-only, focused support, and full multilingual coverage can all be valid;

- language architecture does not create demand or guarantee performance;
- ongoing ownership and page-retirement rules are required.
