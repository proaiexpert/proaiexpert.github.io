## 1. Shared module principles

- The complete article must remain understandable as plain text.
- Visual modules may improve hierarchy but must not remove content or comparison logic.
- Tables must preserve header relationships and source fields.
- Mobile transformations must keep labels attached to values.
- No swipe-only component may be the sole access to information.
- Risk color must always be paired with an explicit text label.
- Sources remain beside the claims they support.
- All material content remains visible without JavaScript.
- Motion, if later approved, must be restrained and support reduced-motion preferences.
- The page shell, navigation, routes, metadata, canonical, `hreflang`, schema, and CTA destination are outside this editorial map and remain governed separately.
