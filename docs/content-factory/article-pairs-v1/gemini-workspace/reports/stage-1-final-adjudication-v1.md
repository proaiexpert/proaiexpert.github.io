## Executive decision

The evidence supplement materially improves the first report and correctly withdraws the unsupported metadata and H1 changes, geographic keyword stuffing, automatic framework assumptions, and overextended YMYL framing.

Stage 1 is sufficient to open a controlled creative-candidate pass.

It is not sufficient to authorize page implementation or to treat every SERP claim as a verified research fact.

## Evidence-quality limitation

The supplement contains a methodological inconsistency:

- it states that live Google Search was used with no model inference;
- it later describes the work as heuristic analysis based on standard U.S. SERP outputs.

Many competitor references are domain-level descriptions rather than exact result URLs, titles, positions, screenshots, or reproducible query captures.

Therefore:

- use the SERP findings as directional research;
- do not use them as a source for precise ranking, market-dominance, search-volume, PAA-frequency, or AI Overview claims;
- do not add such claims to public copy;
- do not change metadata solely because of the supplement.

## Accepted decisions

### Metadata and routes

Keep the authoritative governance manifest unchanged.

Keep all current authoritative H1s, SEO titles, meta descriptions, and routes.

### Geographic strategy

Keep the primary editorial framing national / U.S.-oriented.

Do not force `Seattle` or `Washington` into H1s, titles, descriptions, or slugs.

Washington and Seattle may appear naturally in examples, service context, author perspective, or internal linking where factually relevant.

### Query ownership

Pair 1 owns the business decision among English-only, focused language support, and full multilingual coverage.

Pair 2 owns proposal completeness, scope, responsibility, dependencies, acceptance evidence, document traceability, rights, access, and control.

Do not expand Pair 1 into a full multilingual SEO or Local SEO guide.

Do not expand Pair 2 into pricing methodology, platform comparison, warranties, remedies, termination, indemnification, dispute resolution, or contract templates.

### Creative hypothesis

Accept `The Strategic System` as the preferred direction for Stage 2 exploration, not as a final design decision.

Approved areas to explore:

- decision-system editorial structure;
- stronger openings and narrative tension;
- Broken Journey scenarios;
- Language Coverage Matrix;
- customer-journey continuity from entry page through intake and response;
- Proposal Risk Ledger;
- Business Control Map;
- Definition of Done;
- red-risk decision gate;
- premium evidence modules;
- restrained motion and progressive enhancement.

### Technical direction

Prefer semantic HTML and CSS-first progressive enhancement.

Do not assume React, Vue, MDX, or heavy JavaScript.

Do not replace accessible tables with visual cards unless the full information model, header relationships, comparison ability, keyboard access, and no-JS readability are preserved.

## Rejected or constrained ideas

- No direct move to Stage 3.
- No automatic metadata or H1 changes.
- No claim that the technical baseline is “perfect.”
- No claim that competitors universally ignore operational capacity.
- No hostile wording such as “held hostage by the contractor.”
- No generic fake law-firm, clinic, HVAC, or client story presented as a real example.
- No `Due Diligence Guide`, `audit`, or legal-review positioning unless the service and legal boundary are explicitly approved.
- No promise that structured data or headings will cause AI Overview citation.

- No heavy scrollytelling, 3D decoration, or motion that delays access to the answer.
