## Executive decision

Hybrid V2 corrected most of the factual and legal overstatements identified in Stage 2 V1. The revised Premium Article System Blueprint is suitable as the visual and interaction foundation for Stage 3.

The four Hybrid V2 article files are not approved as standalone replacements for the immutable baseline. They remain substantially shorter than the baseline and omit material decision dimensions that the task explicitly required to restore. The section-retention matrix incorrectly marks several compressed or absent sections as `PRESERVED`.

Stage 3 may proceed only under the approved source hierarchy defined in `stage-3-approved-content-and-design-source-manifest-v1.md`:

1. immutable baseline article = body-content authority;
2. governance manifest = metadata and route authority;
3. Hybrid V2 blueprint = design-system authority;
4. selected Hybrid V2 modules = supplemental presentation modules only.

Do not render the four Hybrid V2 candidates as the complete public article bodies.

## QA-document verdict

### Section-retention matrix

Not accepted as accurate evidence. It labels content as `PRESERVED` when it was summarized into one sentence, replaced by a scenario, or removed.

Examples:

- Pair 1 EN `Translation vs. Localization` is marked `REFRAMED` into the Broken Journey scenario, but those concepts solve different problems.
- Pair 1 RU post-launch governance is marked `PRESERVED` inside one Option C bullet, but the baseline contains a complete operating model.
- Pair 2 RU seven proposal areas are marked `PRESERVED` inside five ledger questions, although the two frameworks are not interchangeable.
- Pair 2 EN normalized A/B/C comparison is absent despite being required.

### Factual and legal QA

Generally useful and accepted as a correction register, but it does not establish that all required baseline methodology was restored.

### Self-evaluation

Not accepted as an objective score. It awards 9–10 while claiming complete restoration that the files do not contain. Use it only as Gemini’s self-assessment, not as an approval record.
