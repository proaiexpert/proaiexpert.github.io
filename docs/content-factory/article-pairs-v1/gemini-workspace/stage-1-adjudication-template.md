## Decision rules

### ACCEPT

The recommendation is factually sound, supports the primary intent, and creates more reader value than risk.

### PARTIAL

The underlying idea is useful, but Gemini must follow the stated limitation.

### REJECT

The recommendation would weaken intent, evidence discipline, voice, pair independence, or implementation quality.

### NEEDS EVIDENCE

Do not use the recommendation as fact until a reliable source or internal evidence is supplied.
