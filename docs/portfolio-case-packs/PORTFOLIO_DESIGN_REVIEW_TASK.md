1. Does the `Cinematic Editorial Systems` direction fit ProAI Expert’s positioning?

3. Is the individual case narrative clear enough for service-business buyers?

5. Is the proposed motion system realistic for desktop and mobile?
6. Are there accessibility, reduced-motion, performance, or SEO risks?
7. Does the shared shell allow Financial Stream, Alina Horb, and Local Repair Pro to feel distinct without looking like three unrelated websites?
8. Is the Financial Stream flagship composition commercially persuasive and evidence-based?
9. What is missing from the prototype approval gate?
10. What exact changes should be made before implementation?

## Required output

Provide one decisive review with:

1. overall verdict;
2. five strongest decisions;
3. five risks or weak points;
4. exact recommended corrections;
5. final approved page structure;
6. final approved animation list;
7. mobile simplification rules;
8. implementation priority order.

Do not rewrite the whole blueprint unless a structural correction is necessary.
Do not recommend animation merely for visual spectacle.
Do not propose fake metrics, testimonials, or client outcomes.
