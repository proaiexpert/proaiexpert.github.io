## 2. Executive decision

The portfolio is already strong. This is not a rebuild-from-zero program.

The next program must:

- elevate the current archive from a strong index into a flagship experience;
- preserve the approved strategy and emotional temperature of each case;
- correct truth, metadata, and navigation inconsistencies;
- improve composition, proof hierarchy, motion restraint, and responsive behavior;
- extend a shared production discipline without forcing one visual template across all projects.

The archive should become the strongest or one of the strongest pages on the ProAI Expert site. It must be more than a directory and more than the sum of the three individual case pages. Its unique job is to reveal the studio method across different business contexts.

Shared consistency applies to:

- navigation and orientation;
- truthful status grammar;
- evidence discipline;
- accessibility;
- performance;
- responsive quality;
- focus states;
- no-JS visibility;
- reduced-motion behavior;
- closing and next-case logic.

Shared consistency does not mean identical art direction, identical chapter architecture, or identical motion.

## 6. Future archive architecture

## 6.1 Three-World Stage Hero

### Purpose

Establish the portfolio as one curated system while keeping all project worlds visible.

### Content

- flagship thesis;
- one concise explanatory line;
- three visual territories;
- project role labels;
- direct case access;

### Visual hierarchy

- Financial Stream uses graphite and restrained teal.
- Alina Horb uses ivory, sage, stone, and muted terracotta.
- Local Repair Pro uses forest, amber, stone, and off-white.
- One territory may gain emphasis on hover, keyboard focus, or tap.

### Responsive behavior

- Desktop: asymmetric three-world composition.
- Tablet: reduced asymmetry with the same logical order.

## 8. Production Standard

## 8.1 Strategy before surface

Visual decisions follow positioning, audience, trust problem, information architecture, and first-step logic.

## 8.2 Truthful framing

Every status and claim must distinguish:

- live;
- implemented;
- tested;
- partial;
- planned;
- in refinement;
- in development;
- concept;
- verified evidence;
- limitation.

## 8.3 Content and information architecture

A premium page must make the decision easier, not merely look expensive.

## 8.4 Bilingual architecture

When multilingual scope applies:

- separate routes;
- natural localized copy;
- localized metadata;
- canonical and reciprocal hreflang;
- x-default;
- language-preserving links;
- proof parity;
- responsive parity.

## 8.5 Responsive production

Mobile is a deliberate recomposition. It is not a smaller desktop layout.

## 8.6 Accessibility

All interactive states must work with keyboard, visible focus, semantic structure, meaningful alternatives, reduced motion, and sufficient contrast.

## 8.7 Performance

Motion and imagery must remain subordinate to fast rendering and stable layout.

## 8.8 Controlled motion

Motion reveals hierarchy, relationship, and state. It must not make reading conditional on animation.

## 8.9 Evidence discipline

Prefer implementation facts, live routes, dated evidence, screenshots, and clearly stated limitations over broad outcome language.

## 8.10 Human-reviewed boundaries

AI or automation must never be framed as replacing professional judgment where the system is human-reviewed, partial, or task-bounded.

## 15. Acceptance criteria

Portfolio Master Polish V1 passes only when:

- the archive is materially stronger than the current version;
- project count is not used as visible positioning;
- all three project worlds remain distinct;
- Financial Stream remains evidence-led and operational;
- Alina remains quiet, human, and editorial;
- Local Repair remains light, practical, and honestly in development;
- Back, Previous, and Next navigation forms a complete loop;
- language switching preserves exact context;
- no truth regression is introduced;
- EN/RU case-page parity is verified, while Alina’s underlying website remains correctly described as UA/RU;
- all core content remains visible without JavaScript;
- reduced-motion behavior is complete;
- keyboard focus is visible;
- headings, landmarks, links, and controls are semantic;
- mobile is checked at 430, 390, 375, 360, and 320 px;
- desktop is checked at representative laptop and large-screen widths;
- no horizontal overflow exists;
- screenshots remain readable and properly captioned;
- motion supports hierarchy and does not block reading;
- LCP, CLS, TBT, and asset loading remain within the project performance budget;
- all metadata, canonical, hreflang, structured data, and social images are checked;
- visual review occurs before merge;
- implementation phases remain isolated and reviewable.
