## 1. Executive design decision

### Decision

Build the case around one concept: **Structured Trust / Доверие по системе**.

The page will be a proof-led editorial experience that shows how a bilingual accounting and tax-support website became a five-layer system for trust, client self-selection, structured intake, content/search visibility, and human-reviewed automation. It will not present the project as a gallery of attractive screens. Each visual moment must reconcile a business problem with a real interface, a dated proof point, or an explicit status.

**One-sentence thesis:** A cinematic but restrained case-study dossier in which scattered, trust-sensitive client situations are progressively organized into one visible, bilingual operating system.

### Why this is the strongest direction

- It preserves the recognizable ProAI Expert shell: near-black surfaces, cyan signal color, Inter, large editorial type, systems language, fixed navigation, and precise technical detailing.
- It gives Financial Stream its own project world: midnight navy, ice-white proof surfaces, restrained green status signals, and a document/ledger rhythm appropriate to a financial-services business without resembling a fintech dashboard template.
- It makes the work legible as strategy and system design, not only website production.
- It uses actual live-site captures as evidence and separates them from GSC, SERP, and automation evidence.
- It creates one reusable case-page grammar for later projects while keeping the Financial Stream art direction specific.
- It is materially stronger than the current ProAI site because orientation, evidence, responsive behavior, motion limits, and claims governance are designed into the page rather than added afterward.

### Primary principles

**Design principle:** every chapter must pair a strategic claim with a visible source of truth.

**Interaction principle:** scrolling may change emphasis, never availability. All essential content remains present, readable, and correctly ordered without animation or JavaScript.

**Evidence principle:** presentation screenshots show the experience; dated evidence proves only the narrowly stated fact; status labels distinguish what is live, implemented, tested, partial, or planned.

### Emotional progression

1. **Authority:** a dark ProAI opening signals a serious live client project.
2. **Credibility:** a route-matched real Financial Stream homepage appears immediately.
3. **Recognition:** the page names the confusion, document risk, and service ambiguity clients bring before a call.
4. **Clarity:** five layers assemble into a comprehensible system.
5. **Inclusion:** EN and RU are shown as parallel service experiences, not a translation afterthought.
6. **Control:** service choices and structured intake reduce uncertainty before booking.
7. **Depth:** content and search evidence show that the system extends beyond a homepage.
8. **Honesty:** automation is useful but visibly bounded by status and human review.
9. **Human validation:** the owner testimonial provides a calm emotional release.
10. **Measured confidence:** dated signals are stated precisely, without converting SEO evidence into invented business outcomes.
11. **Action:** the ProAI shell returns and offers the live project and a relevant next step.

### Strengths to preserve

- The black/cyan shell is distinctive and already associated with ProAI Expert.
- The cube mark and systems language communicate technical capability.
- Large typography gives confidence and a contemporary studio character.
- Fixed navigation, generous width, and alternating dark surfaces support cinematic pacing.
- The current Financial Stream module already uses real work and an owner quote rather than synthetic imagery.
- EN/RU routing and mirrored site structure are already part of the brand’s operating model.

### Weaknesses to correct

- The current system sometimes equates sophistication with continuous motion, 3D transforms, blur, and glow.
- Small uppercase navigation and muted text can fall below a comfortable reading standard.
- Device shells and browser-like frames make real interfaces feel like portfolio decoration.
- Repeated orbits, cubes, cards, and reveal effects compete with proof rather than explaining it.
- The existing case page is a short article with only limited visual proof and outdated broad claims.
- The prototype’s right-side dot rail is cryptic and disappears on tablet without an equivalent orientation aid.
- Large inline style/script surfaces and layered overrides increase implementation and regression risk.
- Wide desktop screenshots become unreadable when compressed into phone-sized decorative frames.
- Existing motion relies too heavily on hover/pointer behavior and full-height sections.

### Preserve, evolve, retire

**Preserve:** `#050506`, cyan signal, Inter, cube/logo shell, large editorial headlines, slim borders, blurred header, restrained grid, bilingual switch, and clear primary CTA.

**Evolve:** the dark shell into alternating project chapters; dots into labelled chapter navigation; generic glass cards into evidence sheets; orbit diagrams into a document-like system stack; device mockups into border-only figures; reveal-on-scroll into progressive emphasis.

**Retire for this page:** metallic device chrome, pointer parallax, draggable phones, cursor-reactive transformations, marquees, constant orbit rotation, autoplay motion, tiny dots as the only navigation, animated metric counting, and any decorative effect that requires a high-end device to feel complete.

## 4. Previous Financial Stream research synthesis

### The audience is problem-led, not service-taxonomy-led

Prospects may know they have messy books, a notice, payroll documents, a filing deadline, or an incomplete business setup without knowing the correct service label. The case therefore needs to show how the site translates situations into service paths instead of merely listing offerings.

### Trust must be built before contact

Accounting and tax-support requests involve risk, uncertainty, sensitive documents, and unfamiliar terminology. Trust is created through clear scope, predictable next steps, safety guidance, bilingual comprehension, and visible human review—not through visual polish alone.

### Intake is part of the product

The historical pack correctly identified that the first interaction determines downstream efficiency. A context-free call can shift discovery work into an expensive synchronous conversation. A structured request makes the situation, business type, documents, timing, and likely service visible first; the short form remains an accessible secondary path; booking follows when context exists.

### Bilingual architecture is operational, not cosmetic

EN/RU affects navigation, service naming, educational content, search entry points, form comprehension, reassurance, and team handling. The public case must therefore show paired real interfaces and natural independent copy, not a language toggle as a feature bullet.

### Content is a service and search layer

Materials and informational routes help users understand documents, notices, services, and next steps before contact. Search evidence proves dated discoverability signals only; it does not prove leads, revenue, conversion, or ROI.

### Automation requires status and human boundaries

Chatbase is live. Gmail + Make + OpenAI drafting is implemented with human review. Twilio/Make missed-call and SMS routing remains tested/partial without fresh end-to-end proof. CRM, autonomous communication, reporting/ROI, and fully stable missed-call recovery remain planned or unverified. The case will make these differences visually unmistakable.

### The narrative implication

The strongest story is not “we redesigned a website.” It is:

> A trust-sensitive, bilingual service business needed a clearer way to turn uncertain questions into informed next steps. ProAI Expert organized that journey across presentation, service choice, intake, content/search, and carefully bounded automation.

### Historical material deliberately not imported

- Payroll removal or deprecation
- 3.88K impressions as a current figure
- 41 indexed pages as a current figure
- outdated screenshot recapture requirements
- unresolved-mobile assumptions
- full-production Twilio wording
- guaranteed local ranking language
- lead, conversion, revenue, ROI, or time-saving claims without evidence

### Principles that materially improve this page

1. **Give orientation before immersion.** The visitor sees project status, proof strip, and a labelled chapter system before the page becomes long.
2. **Let real work carry the art direction.** Screenshots are large, flat, and readable; ornamental frames remain secondary.
3. **One chapter, one job.** Each chapter answers one strategic question and hands off deliberately to the next.
4. **Make evidence grammar consistent.** Every metric has value, unit, period/date, source type, and interpretive limit.
5. **Use sticky behavior for comparison or sequence only.** Sticky is limited to the five-layer model, intake walkthrough, and optional screenshot narrative at large/comfortable viewports.
6. **Treat mobile as a re-authored reading sequence.** No miniature desktop, no swipe-only proof, no hidden chapter orientation.
7. **Status is content.** “Implemented,” “tested/partial,” and “planned” are visually and semantically different.
8. **Footnote precision beats inflated impact language.** Dated search signals are presented confidently without claiming business causality.

### Benchmark conclusion

The premium benchmark does not justify more effects. It justifies more editorial control: a strong opening, readable real work, explicit chaptering, consistent proof, and motion that marks state changes. The Financial Stream page should feel more resolved than the current ProAI site because fewer visual devices are doing more strategic work.

## 6. Final concept

### Concept name

**Structured Trust / Доверие по системе**

### Concept thesis

The case behaves like a carefully reconciled dossier. It begins in the ProAI studio world, opens into Financial Stream’s bright client-facing world, then progressively aligns five layers around one outcome: a person with an uncertain financial situation can understand the scope, choose a relevant path, share useful context safely, and reach the next human step with less ambiguity.

### Visual metaphor: the evidence spine

A thin cyan “evidence spine” begins beneath the hero status line and reappears as a quiet chapter marker. In the system chapter it resolves into five aligned horizontal layers—a reconciliation stack inspired by documents, ledgers, and organized records, but never styled as a fake financial dashboard. In evidence chapters, the spine becomes a source/date rule beneath metrics. In the closing CTA, it returns to the ProAI cyan line.

The spine is a compositional motif, not a literal line continuously animated down the whole page. It is allowed to disappear. This avoids turning the case into a gimmick or creating scroll-linked performance debt.

### Page silhouette

1. **Dark studio opening:** status, editorial H1, route-matched live homepage proof.
2. **Compact proof threshold:** five factual project signals.
3. **Ice-white problem chapter:** client uncertainty is named in plain language.
4. **Midnight system chapter:** five-layer reconciliation stack activates.
5. **Light bilingual chapter:** two real portrait-mobile editions appear side by side.
6. **Dark service chapter:** seven active directions are grouped into three comprehensible families.
7. **Ice-white intake chapter:** real structured-request proof stays visible beside the contact sequence.
8. **Light content/search chapter:** paired Materials screens lead into dated search evidence.
9. **Midnight automation chapter:** one real Chatbase capture and an honest status matrix.
10. **Calm ice testimonial:** owner voice, no competing visuals.
11. **White verified-signals chapter:** exact metrics and limits.
12. **Black ProAI return:** live-site CTA, ProAI CTA, and conditional next-case handoff.

### Why it fits Financial Stream

- The document/record rhythm feels appropriate to accounting and tax support without borrowing banking tropes.
- Ice-white proof surfaces echo the live Financial Stream interface.
- Ordered layers make a broad service scope comprehensible.
- The bilingual comparison is visible, not asserted.
- Safety, context, and human review are treated as value, not friction.

### Why it fits ProAI Expert

- It preserves the black/cyan studio shell and systems-led tone.
- It evolves the existing motion language from ambient spectacle to stateful explanation.
- It demonstrates strategy, UX, content, automation, evidence discipline, accessibility, and implementation thinking in one flagship story.
- It creates reusable case components without making every future project look like Financial Stream.

### How it is stronger than the current site

- A named, labelled chapter navigator replaces a disappearing dot rail.
- Real screenshots lose fake browser/device chrome and receive precise captions.
- Evidence, claims, and automation status are governed by visible rules.
- Sticky storytelling has defined eligibility and fallback conditions.
- Mobile changes narrative order and media treatment instead of merely stacking desktop columns.
- Page length is controlled by alternating dense and quiet chapters; only three chapters may become sticky.
- No critical content depends on hover, scroll progress, or JavaScript.

### Resolved self-critique

The direction was reduced after testing it against the master-task critique gate:

- **Too similar to current ProAI:** corrected through light evidence chapters, flat figures, project-specific navy/ice palette, and the reconciliation stack.
- **Visually strong but strategically weak:** corrected by requiring a business question, source of truth, and transition purpose for every chapter.
- **Too many screenshots:** eight of ten masters are assigned to public V1 across the EN/RU pair; two weaker RU files are reserve.
- **Unreadable screenshot text:** wide desktop frames have minimum display sizes, mobile focal treatments, captions, and text equivalents; screens are omitted from a mobile composition if they cannot stay legible.
- **Repetitive bilingual proof:** EN/RU pairs appear only when comparison is the point.
- **Excessive length/sticky behavior:** no chapter is full-height by default; sticky is limited by width and viewport height and disabled on tablet/mobile/short screens.
- **Weak accessibility/overreliance on JavaScript:** the source order is complete, all diagrams have semantic lists, and no-JS shows the entire story.
- **Unsupported outcomes:** search and indexing remain dated signals, not business results.
- **Partial automation styled as complete:** status, color, label, and copy all differ.
- **Financial Stream overwhelms ProAI:** ProAI owns the shell, chapter system, opening, closing, and component grammar; the project palette lives inside the case.
- **Non-scalable design system:** screenshot, evidence, metric, testimonial, status, navigation, and metadata patterns are reusable; the document metaphor and palette are not forced onto later cases.

Color never carries status alone. Every status includes a word and a distinct icon/shape: filled dot for live/implemented, half-filled square for tested/partial, outline diamond for planned.

### Typography

- **Font:** existing Inter stack only. No new licensed font dependency and no font-file request.
- **Case H1:** desktop 72–124px, line-height 0.88–0.94, tracking approximately `-0.055em`; mobile 48–68px, line-height 0.94–1.0. Russian is allowed an extra line and slightly smaller top size.
- **Chapter H2:** desktop 48–88px, line-height 0.92–1.0; mobile 36–52px.
- **H3:** desktop 26–40px; mobile 24–32px.
- **Lead:** 20–24px desktop, 18–20px tablet, 17–18px mobile.
- **Body:** 17–18px desktop, 16–17px mobile; line-height 1.55–1.7.
- **Caption/source:** 13–14px with 1.45 line-height. Never use 10px for essential evidence context.
- **Eyebrow/status:** 11–12px uppercase with restrained tracking; mobile 11px minimum.
- **Metric value:** 48–84px depending on length; units and period remain adjacent and readable.

Maximum paragraph measure is 68–74 characters. Large display headings use sentence case rather than all caps; status and source labels may use uppercase.

### Widths and grid

- Outer ProAI shell: maximum 1600px.
- Primary case grid: maximum 1440px inside the shell.
- Desktop: 12 columns; 24px gaps; 40px outer padding.
- Tablet: 8 columns; 20px gaps; 24–32px outer padding.
- Mobile: 4 columns; 16px gaps; 16px outer padding at 320–390 and 20px at 430.
- Reading column: 680–760px.
- Primary screenshot panel: maximum 1240px.
- Evidence/metrics field: maximum 1120px.
- Testimonial: maximum 1120px, with readable line lengths enforced through responsive type.

### Spacing rhythm

Use an 8px base with preferred steps of 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 88, 120, 160.

- Desktop chapter padding: 120–160px; hero may be full viewport but not less than content height.
- Tablet chapter padding: 88–112px.
- Mobile chapter padding: 72–88px.
- Quiet testimonial: up to 144px desktop to create a pause.
- Dense proof strip: 24–32px.
- Chapter heading-to-content gap: 48–72px desktop, 32–40px mobile.

### Surfaces, borders, radii, and shadows

- Screenshot figures: 1px structural border, 8px inset on dark or no inset on light, 18px radius, no browser bar, no fake ports, no fake device reflection.
- Evidence sheets: 1px line, 20px radius, solid or 92–96% opaque background; translucency is not used behind small text.
- System cards: 20–24px radius; the active layer gains border/color contrast, not a large scale transform.
- Buttons retain a more ProAI-like 4–8px radius, distinguishing actions from content cards.
- Pills are limited to project status, proof labels, and automation status.
- Shadows are broad and low: approximately 0 24px 80px at 22–35% black on dark, 0 18px 50px at 10–16% navy on light.
- Cyan glow is allowed only on the active chapter indicator, focus ring, or evidence spine; never around every card.

### Gradient and texture

- Dark chapters use a controlled radial ice-blue field positioned behind the active proof, plus a near-black/navy linear base.
- Light chapters use flat ice/white surfaces with a faint 1px ledger grid at 3–4% opacity.
- Noise, if retained, must be a tiny reusable asset or CSS texture already present, under 2% opacity, and removed in high-contrast/reduced-data contexts where practical.
- No animated grain, canvas, WebGL, or full-screen video.

### Screenshot treatment

- A screenshot is always inside a semantic figure with a visible caption.
- The full capture remains the source of truth. Responsive focal cropping may change the visible window but must not distort, recolor, retouch, or fabricate content.
- Critical facts visible inside screenshots are restated in text; a screenshot is never the sole carrier of essential information.
- EN/RU pairs share equal visual weight and explicit language labels.
- Wide desktop captures are not squeezed below their readable threshold in the primary flow. Mobile uses a route-appropriate focal window, an optional full-capture link, or a text equivalent.
- PNG masters remain untouched. Implementation creates optimized WebP delivery derivatives only.

### Metric and evidence styling

Each metric block has five layers of information in this order:

1. Value and unit.
2. What was measured.
3. Period or snapshot date.
4. Source type and review/update date.
5. Interpretation limit.

Numbers never animate upward. Source/date text is visible by default, not hidden in a tooltip. Current GSC performance and indexing use separate cards and separate citations.

## 10. Five-layer system visualization

### Static information architecture

The visualization is an ordered explanatory model with five layers:

1. **Trust and presentation** — clear positioning, professional visual language, scope, reassurance, live EN/RU entry points.
2. **Services and client self-selection** — seven active service directions, situation-led paths, “Not sure” escape route.
3. **Structured intake** — structured request, context review, document/access clarification, short form, calendar after context.
4. **Content and search** — Materials hubs, articles, local/informational routes, dated search and indexing evidence.
5. **AI and automation** — live Chatbase, implemented human-reviewed draft workflow, tested/partial telephony routing, and clearly planned layers.

### Visual metaphor: reconciliation stack

Five horizontal “record sheets” sit slightly offset in depth around one vertical cyan evidence spine. Each sheet has:

- a number `01–05`;
- layer name;
- a one-line user need;
- one proof chip;
- a status marker only where status is relevant.

At rest, all five sheets are legible. The active sheet does not grow dramatically; it aligns with the spine, gains a brighter border, and moves no more than 8px. The metaphor suggests disorganized information being aligned into a usable record. It does not depict money flows, bank balances, charts, or client software.

### Desktop interaction

- Eligibility: viewport at least 1100px wide and 720px high.
- The diagram occupies a sticky left field no taller than 62vh.
- Five layer articles scroll normally on the right.
- When an article heading crosses the central reading band, the corresponding sheet becomes active.
- A local layer index above the articles uses real text labels, not dots. Activating a label scrolls to the related heading using the browser’s normal scrolling behavior.
- The current label may use `aria-current` semantics if technically appropriate; active appearance is never the only indication of reading order.
- The sticky diagram releases before the next chapter, with generous clearance from the fixed header.

### Labels and proof chips

| Layer | User need | Proof chip | Status treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trust and presentation | “Can I understand and trust this business before contact?” | `Live EN/RU homepage` | Live |
| Services and self-selection | “Which path fits my situation?” | `7 active directions` | Live |
| Structured intake | “What context is needed before a call?” | `Structured request first` | Live |
| Content and search | “Can I learn and find a relevant entry point?” | `EN/RU Materials + dated GSC` | Live evidence with dates |
| AI and automation | “Where can assistance reduce friction safely?” | `Chatbase + human review` | Mixed: implemented / tested / planned |

### Mobile and tablet fallback

- The diagram becomes a static vertical stack, then the five full articles follow in the same order.
- Each layer uses a left number, title, one-line need, and proof/status line.
- No sticky region, swipe deck, accordion, carousel, or horizontal panning.
- The mixed automation status is expanded in text rather than compressed into one “AI” label.
- At 320px, the number occupies no more than 32px and the content receives the remaining width.

### Reduced-motion version

- No layer translation, interpolation, or smooth scroll.
- All five sheets remain at equal opacity.
- If active tracking remains, the border/color changes instantly and only after user-initiated navigation or normal intersection observation.
- The entire model remains understandable if active tracking is removed.

### Keyboard behavior

- Normal document headings are the primary navigation targets.
- Local layer links are standard in-page links with visible focus and sufficient offset from the fixed header.
- Focus never moves automatically as the user scrolls.
- No arrow-key-only custom widget is required.
- When a link is activated, focus/reading position lands at the corresponding layer heading without being hidden by sticky navigation.

### Semantic expectation

- One labelled section with an H2.
- One ordered list of five layer articles with H3 headings.
- The diagram is decorative when the ordered list contains the full meaning; otherwise it receives a concise text description and references the same ordered list.
- Proof/status details use visible text, not only icons or color.
- No client UI is fabricated inside the visualization.

### No-JavaScript fallback

All five sheets and all five descriptions display in a static layout. In-page links work through anchors. The chapter remains fully readable, and the rest of the case keeps its correct source order. JavaScript may enhance only the active-state highlight.

### Performance limits

- Prefer lightweight vector/CSS geometry already compatible with the static site.
- No canvas, WebGL, particle field, physics, image sequence, or continuously running animation.
- Scroll observation updates a class/state only; it does not write per-frame transforms.
- The diagram adds no large raster asset and no new framework.

### Chapter navigation specification

- **Wide desktop:** a slim labelled rail inside the 1600px shell, positioned outside the reading column. It displays `04 / 12` and the current short chapter name. An expanded list is available through a clearly labelled control or always-visible text at very wide widths.
- **Compact desktop/tablet:** a sticky chapter bar directly below the global header, showing number and title plus a button to reveal the full chapter list.
- **Mobile:** a 44–48px chapter bar may remain sticky only if it leaves sufficient reading height and never obscures focus. The list opens as a normal dismissible panel/details region; it is not a horizontal chip carousel.
- **Short landscape:** the bar becomes static or reduces to a small progress text in document flow.
- **No-JS:** the full table of contents remains available near the top; fixed active tracking disappears.

### Sticky-storytelling limits

- Maximum three candidate chapters: five-layer system, intake walkthrough, and one evidence/screenshot narrative.
- Only the five-layer and intake chapters are expected to use sticky in V1; the third slot is a contingency, not a target.
- No sticky chapter exceeds roughly 2.6 normal viewport heights.
- Sticky releases at least 80px before the next chapter boundary.
- Sticky is disabled under 1100px width, under 720px height, at 400% zoom/reflow, and whenever it obscures focused content.

### Motion prohibitions

No scroll-jacking, snap-to-section page scrolling, autoplay video, animated cursor, draggable screenshots, perpetual parallax, animated background grain, looping phone tilt, rotating system rings, blur trails, letter-by-letter body copy, or motion that delays access to a claim.

- Status and H1 always precede the screenshot in the DOM and visual order.
- H1 is never forced to a fixed line count. RU may take one additional line.
- CTA group stacks at 320–390 and may sit two across at 430 only if each remains at least 44px high and readable.
- Route-matched homepage master follows the copy. No overlapping phone, floating proof pill, or clipped screenshot under the header.
- The hero is not locked to `100vh` on mobile; use content-driven minimum height and safe-area spacing.

### Header and chapter navigation relationship

- The global ProAI header remains primary and keeps brand, menu, language pair, and contact action.
- The case chapter control is visually quieter and sits below it; both together must not exceed approximately 112px on core mobile.
- Opening one navigation closes or suppresses the other to avoid stacked overlays.
- Exact EN/RU switch maps the two Financial Stream case routes, not the case archive or homepage.
- Focused targets remain visible below both bars.

### Screenshot size and crop

- Portrait mobile masters display at full aspect ratio and never inside fake phones.
- Wide desktop masters are never presented as the sole evidence at an unreadably small size.
- A focal window is allowed only as a presentation of the unchanged image and must retain a full-capture link or adjacent full thumbnail.
- If useful screenshot text cannot remain readable, the screenshot moves out of the primary mobile flow and the chapter uses its semantic text equivalent plus live-route link.
- Captions remain immediately adjacent and use natural EN/RU language.

### Swipe decision

No swipe-only component is used. Bilingual pairs stack; metric cards stack; status bands stack. Horizontal scrolling is reserved only for genuinely two-dimensional media if absolutely necessary, and no planned V1 chapter requires it.

### Evidence and metric cards

- Value, unit, period/date, source, and limitation stay inside one card.
- GSC performance and indexing never collapse into a shared mobile card.
- No tooltip is required to understand a source.
- Cards have at least 20px internal padding at 320 and 24px at 390+.

### Status labels

- Labels wrap rather than truncate.
- Full Russian words are used: `РАБОТАЕТ`, `ВНЕДРЕНО`, `ПРОТЕСТИРОВАНО`, `ЧАСТИЧНО`, `ЗАПЛАНИРОВАНО`.
- Status is repeated in the item heading or first sentence when a chip alone could be missed.

### Tap targets and focus

- Design target: at least 44×44 CSS px for buttons, menu controls, language switch, chapter list items, and expandable full-capture links.
- Inline text links retain adequate line height and spacing.
- Visible focus uses at least a 2px high-contrast perimeter with offset where practical.
- No drag gesture is required; no hover-only explanation exists.

1. Project status and thesis.
2. Route-matched real proof.
3. Verified proof strip.
4. Challenge.

### No-horizontal-overflow rules

- Every grid child must be allowed to shrink; long URLs/terms wrap.
- Decorative glows and lines are clipped inside section containers, not at page level.
- Large type uses responsive limits and emergency wrapping for long RU strings.
- Figures use maximum width 100%; fixed pixel widths never exceed their container.
- Test at 320px and at 400% desktop zoom, not only device presets.

| Owner value statement | Canonical quote approval | Quote verbatim and one natural RU rendering. | Quantified business outcome. |

### Status taxonomy

- **LIVE:** observable on a public route now.
- **IMPLEMENTED:** delivered according to verified project history; may be private or human-mediated.
- **TESTED:** a test occurred; production stability is not asserted.
- **PARTIAL:** some required components or evidence are missing for the full claim.
- **PLANNED:** a future direction or insufficiently verified layer.

Status is attached to the smallest accurate unit. The entire automation chapter is never labelled “Live” merely because Chatbase is live.

### Metric rules

- Always display unit and period beside the value.
- Keep “reviewed” and “last updated” language distinct.

- Do not animate numbers.

- Public derivatives must redact account identity, unrelated properties, search history, names, addresses, emails, phones, message content, tokens, credentials, connection names, webhook IDs, and private URLs.

## 16. Accessibility, SEO and performance requirements

### Accessibility target

Target **WCAG 2.2 Level AA**, following the latest W3C recommendation and using selected stronger practices for focus visibility and motion. W3C organizes WCAG around perceivable, operable, understandable, and robust principles and recommends the latest 2.2 version: [WCAG 2 overview](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/).

#### Structure and reading

- Exactly one H1 per route.
- One main landmark; labelled header/navigation, main, and footer landmarks.
- Twelve chapters are semantic sections with unique H2 headings.
- H3 is used for layer/service/status subdivisions; no skipped hierarchy for visual sizing.
- A skip link reaches main content.
- The table of contents appears in source order and works without JavaScript.
- Figures use captions; evidence sources and dates remain visible text.
- EN document language is English; RU document language is Russian; isolated phrases receive language annotation only when it materially improves pronunciation.

#### Keyboard and controls

- All controls and links work with keyboard alone.
- Chapter navigation uses normal links, not custom drag/scroll widgets.
- Focus order follows reading order and never jumps with scroll activation.
- Focus is never obscured by the global header, chapter bar, sticky figure, or open menu.
- Visible focus target: at least a clear 2px perimeter with 3:1 state contrast where practical, exceeding bare browser-default ambiguity.
- Design target for important controls is 44×44px; at minimum, controls comply with WCAG 2.2 target-size and spacing requirements. W3C’s Level AA minimum is 24×24 CSS px with defined exceptions: [Target Size (Minimum)](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum).
- No critical information exists only on hover, focus, swipe, or scroll position.

#### Reflow and zoom

- Non-exempt content must reflow at 320 CSS px without two-dimensional page scrolling, consistent with [WCAG 1.4.10 Reflow](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html).
- Test 200% text zoom and 400% browser zoom.
- Sticky elements become static when they reduce the usable field or obscure focus/content.
- Long Russian strings, URLs, status labels, and service terms wrap without clipping.
- Diagrams and screenshots may use their own bounded media behavior, but surrounding text and controls must reflow.

#### Contrast

- Normal text: at least 4.5:1.
- Large text: at least 3:1.
- UI boundaries, focus, icons conveying state, and chart/diagram distinctions: at least 3:1 against adjacent colors.
- Muted text tokens must be tested on every dark/light surface; do not assume current alpha values pass.
- Green, amber, and slate status colors require accompanying words and shapes.

#### Motion

- Respect the operating-system reduced-motion preference.
- Remove non-essential translations, smooth scrolling, parallax, and sticky activation motion when reduced motion is requested.
- Provide no unavoidable interaction-triggered motion; W3C specifically warns that unnecessary scroll/parallax motion can harm users with vestibular disorders: [Animation from Interactions](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions).
- No flashing content, auto-playing media, or looping decorative animation.

#### Screenshot accessibility

- Alt text describes what is visibly important in the screenshot and its interface context; the caption states the portfolio claim/source relationship.
- Do not duplicate the entire caption in alt text.
- If adjacent prose already describes all visible meaning, use concise alt text rather than a keyword list.
- Critical steps, metrics, statuses, and safety guidance remain real text outside the image.
- A focal crop must provide a path to the full capture and must not change the claim.
- Screenshots that cannot remain readable are not used as primary mobile information.

### SEO requirements

- Self-referencing canonical on each route.
- Reciprocal `hreflang` for `en` and `ru` on both pages.
- `x-default` points to the EN case route.
- Each language version lists itself and the other version with fully qualified URLs and reciprocal return links, following [Google’s localized-version guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/localized-versions).
- Do not canonicalize RU to EN; each translated body is a distinct, useful language edition.
- Unique EN/RU title, meta description, H1, OG title/description, and localized image alt.
- No automatic language redirect that blocks crawling or user choice.
- Internal links: Case Studies archive, live Financial Stream route for the same language, ProAI contact, and exact paired case route.
- Breadcrumbs are visible and structured accurately; do not mark up routes that do not exist.
- OG/Twitter image: route-specific 1200×630 composition using the real route-matched hero capture and ProAI/Financial Stream titles; no fake interface or metric.
- Meaningful screenshots use indexable image elements and responsive sources with a fallback URL; Google recommends standard image elements, responsive sizing, high quality, and speed-conscious delivery: [Google image SEO best practices](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/google-images).
- Structured-data direction: `WebPage` plus `BreadcrumbList`; represent the case as `CreativeWork` or `Article` only with accurate headline, description, image, author/publisher, date modified, `inLanguage`, about, and main-page relationship. Schema.org defines `inLanguage` using BCP 47 language codes: [CreativeWork](https://schema.org/CreativeWork). No `Review`, `AggregateRating`, fabricated award, or result schema.
- Do not add routes to the production sitemap before source parity, preview approval, and launch approval.

### Performance requirements

Google’s current Core Web Vitals targets are LCP within 2.5 seconds, INP at or under 200ms, and CLS at or under 0.1 at the 75th percentile, segmented by mobile and desktop: [Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals).

#### Route budgets

- No new framework, animation library, canvas, WebGL, or autoplay media.
- Page-specific JavaScript enhancement target: **≤35KB gzip**, excluding a verified shared shell; less is preferred.
- Page-specific CSS target: **≤45KB gzip**, excluding a verified shared shell; consolidate rather than layer overrides.
- Above-fold route-matched WebP target: approximately **≤220KB mobile** and **≤400KB desktop**, subject to visual QA.
- Initial mobile transfer target before user interaction: **≤1.2MB**, with below-fold images lazy-loaded.
- Full page transfer after all ten/evidence media are intentionally viewed: target **≤4.5MB**; lower if visual quality allows.
- Avoid loading both route hero masters above the fold. Only the current-language hero is eager/high priority.
- Only the LCP/hero image receives loading priority. Every non-hero figure is lazy-loaded.
- All images reserve width/height or an exact aspect ratio to prevent layout shift.
- WebP derivatives are generated from locked PNG masters; PNGs remain source/archive files, not default delivery.
- Responsive image widths match actual display sizes; do not serve 2880px originals to a 320px viewport.
- Use the existing Inter family and only weights actually needed. Preserve system fallbacks; avoid a new font family or excessive preloads.
- No large filter/blur animation on raster screenshots.
- Lab target: Lighthouse Performance 90+ on a mobile profile after stabilization, Accessibility 100 where automated checks apply, SEO 100, and no new console errors. Scores are diagnostic gates, not substitutes for manual testing or field data.
- Main-thread lab target: TBT under 200ms; long tasks over 50ms caused by case behavior must be investigated.
- After launch, compare PageSpeed/CrUX or other approved field data; do not claim a Core Web Vitals pass solely from a lab run.

### Progressive enhancement and no-JS

- Every chapter, metric, status, caption, quote, and CTA is present in the initial document.
- Without JavaScript: static table of contents, static five-layer list, normal-flow screenshots, visible evidence/status, functional links, and no hidden content.
- JavaScript may add current-chapter indication and coarse reveal emphasis only.
- Failure to load enhancement code must not leave opacity at zero, freeze sticky content, or remove navigation.

### Privacy and security

- No raw GSC/account data or private workflow screenshots ship.
- External live-site links clearly leave ProAI where needed.
- No new tracking, chat embed, or third-party automation is added to the case page merely to demonstrate the project.
- Existing analytics/cookie behavior is preserved unless a separate approved task changes it.
