## 5. Visual and editorial system

### 5.1 Surfaces

1. ProAI near-black shell.
2. Financial Stream midnight navy.
3. Ice-white documentary surface.
4. Neutral proof surface for real captures.

Cyan marks an active relationship or verified alignment. Green is reserved for a confirmed operational capability.

### 5.2 Typography

- One H1 per route.
- Editorial display scale with natural language-specific wrapping.
- No forced line-break parity between EN and RU.
- RU may occupy one additional line at standard desktop and up to five natural lines at 320 px.
- Use a readable body measure around `60–68ch` as a starting point, adjusted during preview review for the actual font and language.
- Use a narrower readable measure for captions.
- Monospace is limited to source, date and status metadata.
- Metadata must remain readable and must not become decorative microtype.

### 5.3 Proof surfaces

A screenshot may use:

- one thin border;
- an 8–12 px radius;
- one restrained shadow or proof mat;
- controlled crop;
- near-full-bleed scale.

It must not combine large radius, glass, glow, heavy shadow, thick border and fake browser chrome.

Do not use:

- MacBook frames;
- iPhone frames;
- generic device mockups;
- fake browser chrome;
- synthetic dashboards;
- generated project imagery.

### 5.4 Public source rails versus internal provenance

Public source rails must remain concise and useful to a visitor.

They may include:

- live route or source type;
- language;
- verified capture or evidence date.

They must not expose internal repository terminology such as:

- `V2 candidate`;
- `portfolio master`;
- DPR;
- missing capture-log fields;
- internal handoff names.

Internal file path, dimensions, DPR, retained metadata and missing metadata remain in the screenshot matrix and manifest only.

## 10. Motion and interaction specification

Exactly six motion-effect classes are permitted. A class may appear at multiple approved instances; no seventh effect may be introduced under another name.

### Effect 1 — Evidence Lock

- Purpose: align the first real screenshot with its caption/source register after first paint.
- Initial state: 10–12 px horizontal offset and approximately 0.96 opacity.
- Final state: final alignment and full opacity.
- Desktop timing: 650–720 ms.
- Mobile: 6–8 px; 480–520 ms.
- Easing: `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`.
- Reduced motion / no JS: render final state immediately.
- LCP image must remain visible throughout.

### Effect 2 — Layer Reconciliation

- Purpose: mark the Five-layer record currently in the central reading band.
- Initial to final: active Register mark offset by 4–6 px to aligned mark.
- Timing: 240–300 ms with the same expressive easing.
- Mobile: static ordered stack.
- Reduced motion / no JS: static visible marker.

### Effect 3 — Proof Surface Settle

- Purpose: fix Request, Materials or Reporting proof into its evidence position.
- Initial state: 8–12 px vertical offset and approximately 0.96 opacity.
- Final state: final position and full opacity.
- Timing: 420–500 ms.
- Mobile: 4–6 px and 300–340 ms, or static on constrained devices.
- Reduced motion / no JS: final state immediately.
- Only one large raster may move at a time.

### Effect 4 — Surface Handoff

- Purpose: clarify a change between analytical dark and documentary light chapters.
- Treatment: confined local boundary marker only; content remains visible.
- Timing: 360–440 ms.
- Mobile / reduced motion / no JS: static boundary.
- No full-background scrub.

### Effect 5 — Source Lock

- Purpose: connect an already visible evidence value with its period, source and limitation.
- Initial state: source rule slightly shortened; metadata remains readable at near-final contrast.
- Final state: complete rule and final contrast.
- Timing: 320–380 ms.
- Mobile: static or shortened to 240 ms.
- Reduced motion / no JS: all evidence fully visible.
- Numbers never animate.

### Effect 6 — Register Closure

- Purpose: resolve the evidence system into the final CTA.
- Initial to final: short registration segments offset by 4–8 px to one aligned CTA rule.
- Timing: 480–560 ms.
- Mobile: 280–340 ms or static.
- Reduced motion / no JS: final rule immediately.

### Prohibited motion

- large parallax;
- scroll-linked scale;
- blur or filter animation;
- continuous line drawing;
- animated counters;
- scanlines;
- cursor behavior;
- 3D motion;
- autoplay media;
- generic reveal effects on every heading or card;
- essential content starting at zero opacity.

Reduced-motion implementation suppresses non-essential translation rather than merely shortening it.
