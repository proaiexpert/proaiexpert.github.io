## 5. Required new visual direction

Working direction:

# Structured Trust — Midnight Continuum

The page should feel like one continuous premium dark environment rather than alternating black and white full-width bands.

Required principles:

- approximately 75–85% of the page uses near-black, graphite, midnight navy and deep blue-black surfaces;
- remove the full-page white/dark zebra rhythm;
- light ice or paper tones appear mainly inside proof surfaces, screenshot mats, evidence records or small inset documentary zones;
- use gradual tonal transitions, depth, local illumination, architectural lines and layered surfaces instead of hard full-width color switching;
- keep cyan restrained but visually meaningful;
- reserve green for confirmed live operational status;
- screenshots must feel integrated into the composition, not inserted as repeated rectangular illustrations;
- vary image scale, position, crop and chapter composition;
- do not use the same `copy + screenshot` template repeatedly;
- maintain a clear relationship to the premium black ProAI Expert shell while giving Financial Stream its own midnight-navy identity.
