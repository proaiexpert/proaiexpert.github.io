## 1. Approved evidence

### 1.1 Performance snapshot

Visible facts:

- selected period: **6 months**;
- search type: **Web**;
- total clicks: **57**;
- total impressions: **7.24K**;
- average CTR: **0.8%**;
- average position: **35.2**;
- owner-supplied capture date: **August 4, 2026**.

Safe interpretation:

- this is cumulative performance in the selected six-month window;
- the graph supports a dated search-visibility snapshot;
- it is not a controlled comparison against the previous three-month screenshot.

Do not claim:

- threefold traffic growth;
- an exact period-over-period increase;
- lead, conversion, or revenue growth;
- SEO ROI;
- guaranteed rankings or future performance;
- that impressions are visits.

### 1.2 Page-indexing snapshot

Visible facts:

- report: **Page indexing**;
- scope: **All known pages**;
- indexed: **50**;
- not indexed: **13**;
- non-indexing groups: **4 reasons**;
- last update shown: **7/23/26**.

Safe interpretation:

- 50 pages were indexed at the dated July 23 snapshot;
- 13 known pages were not indexed across four reason groups;
- indexed and excluded totals can change as pages are published, consolidated, redirected, removed, and reprocessed by Google;
- according to the project owner, Financial Stream continues to publish several new practical articles each month.

Do not claim:

- that one fewer indexed page is a loss of performance;
- that fewer excluded pages alone proves technical improvement;
- that all important URLs are indexed;
- that the snapshot guarantees future coverage.
