## What must be improved

The existing case is valid but visually and evidentially too narrow. It currently proves mainly:

1. homepage presentation;
2. service architecture;
3. owner testimonial.

The flagship version must prove six layers:

1. **Brand and trust system** — premium desktop/mobile presentation.
2. **Bilingual system** — paired EN/RU routes and content.
3. **Service architecture** — current service scope, including payroll.
4. **Intake system** — deep request, short form, calendar-after-context.
5. **Content and SEO system** — blog hubs, article pair, local pages, dated GSC evidence.
6. **Automation layer** — Chatbase and sanitized Gmail/Make proof; Twilio labeled partial/tested unless fresh production proof exists.

## Claims policy

Safe:

- real live client project;
- EN/RU website architecture;
- current services including payroll;
- structured and short inquiry paths;
- linked calendar-after-context;
- Chatbase entry point;
- dated GSC metrics with period;
- owner testimonial already published on ProAI Expert;
- Gmail draft workflow after sanitized visual proof.

Not safe without evidence:

- exact conversion increase;
- exact lead increase;
- revenue growth;
- universal search ranking;
- fully autonomous communication;
- fully live Twilio missed-call recovery;
- unsupported time-saved claims.
