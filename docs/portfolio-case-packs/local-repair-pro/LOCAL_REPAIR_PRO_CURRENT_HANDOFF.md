## 4. Locked signature system

**Photo-to-Scope Field System** remains the only Local Repair-specific signature system.

RU public rendering:

**Система «От фото к понятному объёму работ»**

Four steps:

1. `AREA` — wider area and surrounding context;
2. `DETAIL` — relevant defect, fixture, finish or access detail;
3. `PRIORITY` — what matters and what result is expected;
4. `FIT` — service group, location, access, materials and specialist-boundary review.

The system is one signature section inside the case. It is not the entire public positioning of the page.

It does not claim image recognition, AI analysis, photo upload, automatic scope generation, pricing or automatic project acceptance.

Visual expression:

- off-white field-sheet surfaces;
- deep-forest rules;
- restrained amber registration marks;
- large readable labels;
- one local alignment effect;
- no permanent grid;
- no dashboard microtype;
- no Financial Stream-style Evidence Reconciliation Register.

### 8.2 Local Repair project world

Palette:

- deep navy `#102033`;
- deep forest `#243D32`;
- warm amber `#C9822B`;
- accessible dark amber `#9E5F10`;
- warm off-white `#F7F2EA`;
- warm stone approximately `#D8CEC0`;
- graphite proof rail approximately `#151C22`.

Material references:

- matte painted surfaces;
- warm stone;
- restrained paper field notes;
- dark enamel;
- Pacific Northwest atmosphere;
- clean trim and finished-home details.

Do not use literal woodgrain backgrounds, workshop dust, tool textures, rustic signage, glassmorphism, glow-heavy cards, fake browser chrome, device frames or a generic contractor template.

Surface rhythm:

1. black → deep forest;
2. warm stone;
3. off-white structured method;
4. bright off-white / maximum image density;
5. deep forest;
6. warm stone + off-white form;
7. graphite;
8. off-white → canonical black.

### 8.3 Five motion classes

1. **Proof Surface Settle** — Chapters 01, 04, 06 and 07; 4–8 px; starts visibly at approximately `.96` opacity; 440–500 ms desktop, 300–340 ms mobile.
2. **Field Note Lock** — Chapters 02–03 and concise source rails; 4 px local registration; 280–320 ms.
3. **Scope Alignment** — Chapter 03 only; 4–8 px; 520–580 ms.
4. **Surface Handoff** — confined transitions 01→02, 04→05 and 07→08; 360–420 ms.
5. **CTA Closure** — Chapter 08 only; 440–500 ms desktop, 280–320 ms mobile.

Preferred easing:

`cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`

No screenshot starts invisible. No scenario autoplay, continuous rail, blur, parallax, counters, particles, typing, cursor effects, 3D transitions, sticky storytelling or mandatory smooth scrolling.

Reduced-motion and no-JavaScript states show the final composition immediately.

- `1280`: preserve asymmetry; intake crops remain paired only while legible;
- `1024 landscape`: copy before proof; chapter orientation in normal flow; no sticky behavior;
- `768`: one reading column; crops and mobile pair stack when required;
- `430`: natural H1 wrapping; full-width CTAs when needed;
- `390`: M02 presentation with at least 16 px side padding;
- `320`: complete one-column reflow; no negative-margin screenshots or page-level overflow;
- short landscape: no 100vh Hero; disable sticky controls that obscure content;
- 200% zoom: single-axis flow with captions attached;
- 400% / 320-equivalent: no two-dimensional page scrolling.

Accessibility target:

**WCAG 2.2 AA**

Required:

- one H1;
- sequential H2 structure;
- semantic figures and captions;
- live-text equivalents for screenshot meaning;
- visible focus;
- no hover-only evidence;
- no mandatory swipe or autoplay;
- complete reduced-motion and no-JavaScript states;
- approximately 44 × 44 px touch targets;
- exact EN/RU route pairing.

Performance targets for later implementation, not current claims:

- LCP ≤ 2.5 s;
- INP ≤ 200 ms;
