# 14. Motion story

## A. Idle life

Even when no main signal is travelling:

- extremely subtle camera/depth drift;
- different depth planes move by tiny different amounts;
- slow specular travel across selected metal edges;
- internal cyan volume gently breathes;
- tiny particles/data remain alive at different depth levels;
- reflection responds slightly later than the object.

The scene must never feel completely dead.

## B. Information arrival

Several small packets approach from the left.

Vary:

- speed;
- size;
- depth;
- opacity;
- timing.

No single long beam.

## C. Occlusion / real depth cue

This is one of the most important effects.

Packets must:

- pass behind foreground metal;
- disappear because of occlusion;
- reappear through internal glass/deeper layers;
- continue farther into the Core.

This is what creates a convincing 3D illusion.

## D. Internal processing

Inside the Core:

- local pulses;
- glass/refraction response;
- subtle illumination changes;
- several channels converging;
- nearby internal surfaces reacting to passing information;
- depth-dependent particles.

No explosive sci-fi effect.

## E. Collector

Information converges into a controlled right-side processing / collector zone.

Use a restrained pulse to indicate completion.

## F. Four outputs

Collector triggers:

- `01 TRUST`
- `02 INQUIRY`
- `03 RESPONSE`
- `04 OUTCOME`

Use four short spatial impulses tied exactly to the four rail positions.

No spaghetti lines.

## G. Return to idle

After outputs:

- internal light decays naturally;
- small residual data remains alive;
- specular/reflection settles;
- scene returns to idle without a hard reset.
