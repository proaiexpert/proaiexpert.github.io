## C-shape motion choreography — locked direction

Motion must be meaningful and restrained.

### A. Entrance / assembly

On initial load only:

- 2–3 outer architectural planes begin slightly misaligned (approximately 8–12 px equivalent offset);
- they slide into final controlled alignment;
- duration approximately 900–1200 ms;
- physical slow easing;
- no bounce;
- no spin spectacle.

Meaning: fragmented business layers become one controlled system.

### B. Main signal flow

One coherent cyan information signal:

- approaches from Trust / Presence;
- passes Inquiry;
- enters the C-shaped Core through a narrow seam/path;
- visibly activates the internal Intelligence Layer;
- continues outward toward Response;
- finishes at Follow-up.

Do not run four independent random pulses.
Do not make all labels flash.
Do not run the signal constantly at full intensity.

### C. Core breathing / idle

After the entrance sequence, the Hero becomes quiet.

Allowed idle motion:

- very slow yaw approximately ±1–1.5°;
- approximately 14–18 second cycle;
- extremely subtle internal cyan luminance variation;
- optional 1–3 px relative depth shift among structural layers.

No continuous 360° rotation.
No obvious bobbing.
No mechanical wobble.

### D. Pointer interaction — desktop only

- maximum approximately ±2–3° controlled tilt/yaw;
- subtle layer depth response;
- no cursor-follow glow ball;
- no tilt-card gimmick;
- no labels chasing the pointer.

### E. Reduced motion / no-JS

Static assembled Hero must already look complete and premium.

Reduced motion must preserve:

- final object geometry;
- full semantic meaning;
- static signal route;
- readable CTA and copy.

Nothing important may depend on animation.
