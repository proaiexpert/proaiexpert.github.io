### EN

**Request a Private Review**

### RU

## 11. Final ten-block narrative

### Block 01 — Hero

Function:

- identify the audience;
- name the two connected directions;
- state the primary business change;
- present the first action.

The Hero must not carry the full differentiation argument.

Working EN direction:

- eyebrow: `AI SYSTEMS, AUTOMATION & PREMIUM WEBSITES FOR SERVICE BUSINESSES`;
- H1 direction: `Build trust. Handle inquiries. Reduce manual work.`

Working RU direction:

- eyebrow: `AI-СИСТЕМЫ, АВТОМАТИЗАЦИЯ И ПРЕМИАЛЬНЫЕ САЙТЫ ДЛЯ СЕРВИСНОГО БИЗНЕСА`;
- H1 direction: `Выстраиваем доверие. Наводим порядок в обращениях. Сокращаем ручную работу.`

Final production copy requires a dedicated EN/RU copy pass and real-layout approval.

### Block 02 — Connected Business Journey

Merge problem recognition and the signature system into one section.

Structure:

1. three concise breakdown signals: trust friction, inquiry friction, operational friction;
2. one connected before/after journey;
3. one conclusion explaining why website and automation belong together.

The section must be understandable without animation.

Desktop may use a connected system. Mobile must transform into four grouped steps:

1. understand and trust;
2. submit a clear request;
3. route and respond;
4. follow up and improve.

Do not compress a wide eight-stage poster onto mobile.

### Block 03 — Two Core Directions

Each direction communicates:

- one recognizable problem;
- one plain-language intervention;
- one intended practical effect;
- one link to the deeper service page.

Do not reproduce technical service-page matrices.

### Block 04 — Financial Stream Flagship Proof

Financial Stream is the strongest live proof and appears early.

The section tells one evidence story:

- clearer service context before contact;
- structured inquiry before calendar;
- live EN/RU implementation;
- bounded human-reviewed automation;
- one restrained dated evidence line if useful.

Do not reproduce the complete case or turn the Homepage into a metrics dashboard.

### Block 05 — Ways to Start

This is an engagement-entry model, not a package table.

Use three visitor situations:

1. the website and trust path are the primary problem;
2. inquiry handling or repetitive process is the primary problem;
3. both sides need to work as one system.

Each entry states:

- `Best when...`;
- the bounded first objective;
- what the entry does not imply;
- a contextual service link or the primary review CTA.

Ongoing support appears as a continuation after an initial controlled project, not as an equal fourth package.

Do not show Homepage prices.

### Block 06 — How the Work Is Controlled

Use five phases:

1. Review context.
2. Define priorities and boundaries.
3. Build the focused system.
4. Launch and verify.
5. Improve where evidence supports it.

Detailed methodology remains on service and case pages.

### Block 07 — Founder Accountability

Compact founder proof:

- role;
- accountability;
- Washington base;
- strategy, AI systems, automation, and website architecture scope;
- EN/RU/UA working capability;
- links to About and LinkedIn.

### Block 08 — Selected Work

Show:

- Alina Horb;
- Local Repair Pro;
- at most a compact callback to Financial Stream.

Do not repeat the flagship screenshot, thesis, and CTA.

### Block 09 — Selected Insights

Show no more than three decision-support articles:

- website trust or buying decisions;
- practical AI and automation;
- multilingual website strategy.

This is an authority layer, not the main commercial argument.

### Block 10 — Final Conversion

Restate the controlled next step.

One primary CTA. No new service argument after the conclusion.

- leads;

- strong typography;

- decorative 3D spectacle;
- continuous rotation;
- particles;
- repeated equal glass cards;
- technology-logo walls;

- one meaningful signature system;
- real project screenshots;
- intentional surface chapters;
- a large flagship proof field;
- compact founder photography;

## 16. EN/RU parity

Parity means equal:

- business intent;
- service hierarchy;
- proof quality;
- project status;
- CTA function;
- destination context;
- responsive priority;
- form and success-state behavior.

It does not mean literal translation, equal sentence length, or identical line breaks.

Preferred RU terminology:

- `AI-системы и автоматизация`;
- `Премиальные сайты и брендинг`;
- `До обращения`;
- `После обращения`;
- `Запросить первичный разбор`;
- `Смотреть клиентские проекты`;
- `С чего можно начать`;
- `Проверка человеком` or `Решение остаётся за человеком`, depending on context.

Avoid mixed terminology such as `intake-слой`, unexplained `routing`, `deployment`, `follow-up`, or imported English UI labels.

## 18. Production architecture rule

Homepage V2 must not be built by adding another broad layer of Liquid `replace_first`, string splitting, or snapshot patching to the current wrappers.

Requirements:

- create a clean V2 source architecture;
- preserve current snapshots as a recoverable rollback source during release;
- use structured EN/RU content ownership;
- keep Header and Footer includes unchanged;
- maintain canonical, hreflang, x-default, metadata, route, and sitemap integrity;
- define exact CSS, JavaScript, image, and data ownership before coding;
- use progressive enhancement only;
- verify a no-JS baseline.

The approved implementation architecture is defined in the separate Homepage V2 Production Specification.
