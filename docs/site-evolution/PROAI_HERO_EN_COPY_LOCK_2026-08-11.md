# 2. Final H1 decision

Selected:

`From first impression to result — one system.`

The English Hero deliberately mirrors the Russian architecture and meaning rather than using the prior `FOLLOW-THROUGH` variant. `RESULT` keeps the four-stage system compact, one-word, visually symmetric, and semantically aligned with the Russian `РЕЗУЛЬТАТ` and the H1 endpoint.

---

# 3. Final CTA decision

Selected primary CTA:

`Request a Review`

`Private` is removed because privacy is implicit in an individual review and the extra word adds length without enough meaning. `Request a Review` is shorter, cleaner, and closely matches the Russian `Запросить разбор`.
