## 3. Chosen architecture

The selected direction is:

# HYBRID CONNECTED SYSTEM CORE

- calm editorial copy on left;

## 4. Strong locked direction from synthesis

Preserve:

- DOM + WebGL hybrid;
- custom 3D Core, not default cube;
- graphite/gunmetal/cold steel;
- cyan as internal intelligence/light;
- one seamless environment;
- no orbit rings;
- no crypto/gaming/Tron;
- no particle soup;
- no broad bloom;
- no literal cables/arrows/laser flowchart;
- no fast rotation or camera circus;
- stages read as a journey, not features;
- static composition must work before motion;
- mobile is separately composed;
- reduced-motion is designed, not an afterthought.

## 5. Recommended Core synthesis

Use a **precision-machined contained instrument** derived from the cube/Core lineage:

- clear simple silhouette;
- restrained chamfer/bevel language;
- one major inset/aperture system rather than many random geometry operations;
- subtle directional asymmetry to support intake → processing → output;
- one meaningful smoked-glass/intelligence aperture if it proves visually and technically valuable;
- contained internal cyan volume;
- rare physically plausible warm/champagne edge reflection only, not a third brand color;
- pearlescence optional and microscopic only;
- strong bevel highlights + controlled environment reflection are higher priority than exotic shaders.

## 6. Recommended stage synthesis

Four stages remain.

Do not use symmetric four-corner orbit or equal square dashboard cards.

Preferred direction:

- wide editorial spatial plates;
- strong 01–04 typography;
- heading + one short line;
- asymmetric/directional progression around/across the Core;
- one active stage at a time;
- inactive stages remain legible but calm;
- no excessive glassmorphism;
- no icons unless they add proven meaning.
