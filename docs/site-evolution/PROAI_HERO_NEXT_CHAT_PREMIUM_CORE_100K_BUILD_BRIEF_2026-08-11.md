# 2. Core creative decision — do not invent another simple box

The rejected R1 “machined box / expensive cube” visual is **NOT** the art-direction reference.

Do not reuse its geometry merely because it is already implemented.

The next direction is:

## **SCULPTURAL SYSTEM CORE + ABSTRACT SIGNAL FIELD**

The starting DNA is the older/current ProAI 3D Hero concept:

- one dominant central 3D Core;
- spatial depth;
- visible motion/life;
- cyan intelligence/energy;
- surrounding paths/signals;
- four-stage system narrative.

The goal is to upgrade this DNA by several quality levels, not replace it with a simpler design.

The owner explicitly wants a strong visual style. Premium quality here includes visual impact, custom 3D authorship, motion, material quality, atmosphere and memorable composition — not only restraint and usability.

---

# 3. What the new Core should be

The Core should feel like a bespoke ProAI object/system that could not be mistaken for a stock Three.js primitive.

Recommended form language:

- retain a subtle cube/monolithic geometric ancestry so it still feels connected to the old Hero;
- evolve it into an **asymmetric sculptural object**, not a closed rectangular box;
- use 3–5 interlocking/floating shell masses, frames, plates or folded surfaces around one coherent inner intelligence volume;
- create meaningful negative space and one or two openings/channels through the form;
- show physical depth, shell thickness, bevels/chamfers and controlled edge highlights;
- allow one smoked-glass / dark-transmission aperture if it genuinely improves the object;
- internal cyan light should sit deep inside the Core and feel contained, layered and intelligent;
- geometry should subtly suggest `input → processing → output`, but not become literal machinery, a server, reactor, circuit board or flowchart;
- the silhouette must remain strong before any glow or post-processing is added.

### Logo integration

A ProAI mark/logo may be integrated into one selected surface if it looks authored and premium.

Preferred treatments:

- subtle engraving;
- inset mark;
- embedded metal/glass badge;
- very restrained internal illumination.

Do **not** apply the logo as a flat sticker/decal that makes the object look like a mockup.

---

# 4. Surrounding abstraction — upgrade, do not merely delete

The older Hero used obvious dotted ellipses/orbit rings. The owner does not want a blanket removal of motion or effects; he wants these elements redesigned to a much higher level.

The new surrounding system should behave like an **Abstract Signal Field**.

Instead of simple concentric dotted ovals, use a composed spatial family such as:

- 2–4 non-concentric 3D spline trajectories with different depth planes and irregular curvature;
- partial arcs that enter and disappear behind the Core;
- paths that transition between fine continuous line, broken micro-segments, soft luminous data marks and faint spatial dust only where useful;
- variable opacity, thickness, frequency and material response along a path;
- perspective occlusion and depth so trajectories feel embedded in space rather than drawn on top of the webpage;
- occasional branching/merging or controlled phase transitions to suggest information becoming organized;
- local cyan highlights instead of uniformly glowing neon rings;
- some trajectories may visually originate near the inquiry side, pass through/behind the Core, then resolve toward response/result.

The field should feel **parametric, spatial and authored**, not like 1990s dotted orbit graphics and not like a generic crypto/Tron HUD.

No element is forbidden merely because it is a line, glow, particle, arc or orbit. The test is quality, meaning and integration.

# 5. Four-stage journey

The stage narrative is locked:

RU: `ДОВЕРИЕ → ОБРАЩЕНИЕ → ОТВЕТ → РЕЗУЛЬТАТ`

EN: `TRUST → INQUIRY → RESPONSE → RESULT`

Preferred treatment:

- each stage can use a strong `01–04` numeral + label, with microcopy only if it remains visually premium;

The stages and the Core must read as **one composition**, not text cards floating around an unrelated object.

# 6. Motion — visually rich is allowed and expected

Do NOT interpret “premium” as “almost static”.

High-end digital sites frequently use strong 3D movement, camera response, animated materials and layered motion. The objective is not to minimize movement; it is to make movement authored, coherent and expensive.

### Core motion

Allowed and encouraged when it looks intentional:

- slow continuous orientation drift / rotation;
- multi-axis movement with changing speed/easing rather than a default constant 360° demo spin;
- occasional controlled reorientation to reveal another face/opening/logo;
- subtle internal counter-motion or nested rotation;
- micro movement of shell layers/plates;
- tiny breathing/parallax response;
- stage-driven orientation/state changes.

A continuously moving object is **not automatically a problem**. A generic default rotating cube is the problem.

### Signal-field motion

Possible techniques:

- signal pulses travelling through spline trajectories;
- segments that organize/resolve as they approach or exit the Core;
- local line growth/dissolve;
- depth-aware particles or data marks appearing only in selected beats;
- path phase transitions from fragmented input to cleaner output;
- very subtle rotational/precessional motion in the surrounding field.

### Narrative motion

A strong baseline choreography:

1. Hero is immediately readable; copy is never hidden behind a long intro.
2. Core emerges from darkness through material/light definition rather than a cheap scale-pop.
3. Signal Field establishes spatially.
4. `01 TRUST` becomes active; the scene reads as first impression / presence.
5. `02 INQUIRY` activates; fragmented signals/context enter the Core.
6. Internal cyan intelligence becomes more structured/organized.
7. `03 RESPONSE` activates; output resolves cleaner/faster on the far side.
8. `04 RESULT` activates; the Core + field settle into the most coherent completed state.
9. Scene continues in a sophisticated idle rather than becoming dead/static.

The sequence may replay subtly or allow hover/focus/tap stage activation if the final behavior remains comfortable and premium.

Do not impose a blanket ban on loops, rotation or continued motion. Evaluate the actual visual result.

# 7. Material and lighting target

Primary world:

- near-black seamless Hero environment;
- graphite / gunmetal / dark steel;
- smoked transparent/translucent elements only where meaningful;
- cyan/ice-blue as internal intelligence and signal energy;
- controlled silver highlights;
- rare warm/champagne environmental reflection is allowed as physical material nuance, not as a third brand color;
- subtle pearlescent/view-dependent response may be tested if it stays microscopic and sophisticated rather than rainbow/iridescent.

The object must not read as black plastic with cyan bloom.

High-ROI visual work:

- authored geometry;
- proper bevels and normals;
- excellent PBR roughness/metalness variation;
- premium environment reflections;
- internal depth/light layering;
- selective transmission/refraction;
- Fresnel/specular response;
- precise contact/grounding and atmospheric integration;
- antialiasing and render stability.

Glow/bloom is allowed selectively when it reads as optical light spill, not as a giant neon outline.

# 9. Implementation architecture

Preferred architecture remains hybrid:

### DOM owns

- header/nav;
- eyebrow;
- H1/support;
- CTA + microcopy;
- stage typography;
- responsive layout;
- accessibility.

### Three.js/WebGL owns

- Core geometry;
- material system;
- Signal Field trajectories;
- internal light/intelligence;
- scene depth;
- camera/light motion;
- premium effects.

Important text must not be baked into WebGL.

Use existing site architecture and avoid adding a framework only for the Hero unless truly necessary.

Performance/reduced-motion/mobile still matter, but they are engineering requirements, **not reasons to impoverish the desktop art direction**. Build graceful degradation and mobile simplification separately.
