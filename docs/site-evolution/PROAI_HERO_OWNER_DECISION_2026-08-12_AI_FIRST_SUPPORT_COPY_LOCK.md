## 1. POSITIONING DECISION

The Hero should present ProAI Expert as an integrated business-systems company where:

- AI systems and automation are a primary operating layer;
- premium websites are the client-facing trust and inquiry layer;
- the offer is one connected system rather than a web studio with AI added afterward.

Directional perception target:

- approximately 55–60% AI systems / automation;
- approximately 40–45% premium websites.

This is an art-direction / positioning target, not a literal quantified marketing claim.
