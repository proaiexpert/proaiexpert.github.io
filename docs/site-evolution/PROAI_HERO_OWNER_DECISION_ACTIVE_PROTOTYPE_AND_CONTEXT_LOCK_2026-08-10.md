## 1. Immediate owner decision

The currently running Hybrid Connected System Core prototype is **not to be stopped**.

It may finish as an exploratory prototype because it may contain useful visual, motion, material or technical ideas.

However:

- its output is **not automatically the final Hero direction**;
- its design decisions are **not automatically owner-approved**;
- it must not overwrite the external-reviews synthesis as strategy authority;
- it must not modify production;
- it must not merge/deploy/open a production PR.

After the prototype is complete, it will be reviewed against the preserved synthesis and owner intent. Useful elements may be kept; weak elements may be revised or discarded.

## 2. Chosen strategic direction remains unchanged

The chosen architecture is:

# HYBRID CONNECTED SYSTEM CORE

This means:

- calm editorial copy on the left;
- one seamless near-black Hero environment;
- a real bespoke 3D System Core built with Three.js/WebGL;
- four real DOM journey stages;
- one connected business-system narrative;
- premium material credibility instead of VFX quantity;
- meaningful restrained motion;
- deliberate mobile recomposition;
- designed reduced-motion behavior.

The archived C-shape is historical R&D only and is not reopened.

## 5. Important creative latitude — do NOT over-lock prematurely

The synthesis is **not** a checklist that must be implemented mechanically.

Do not reduce the Hero to a rigid collection of rules before seeing the whole composition.

The next owner review should judge the complete experience first:

1. Does the H1 remain the verbal authority?
2. Does the Core feel bespoke, physically credible and premium?
3. Does the scene communicate one connected system?
4. Do the four stages feel sequential rather than like SaaS feature cards?
5. Does motion clarify the story rather than merely decorate it?
6. Does the entire Hero feel authored and distinctive rather than assembled from effects?

Creative experimentation is allowed **inside the chosen Hybrid Connected System Core architecture**.

The following remain legitimate creative variables and should not be prematurely banned solely because reviewers disagreed:

- exact Core silhouette/geometry;
- amount and form of bevel/chamfer/inset treatment;
- one meaningful smoked-glass aperture/chamber if it improves the object;
- subtle directional asymmetry to imply intake → processing → output;
- exact arrangement of the four spatial plates;
- restrained internal light propagation;
- whether a transient signal trace is useful after testing the scene without it;
- microscopic blue-steel / warm-champagne edge response;
- microscopic pearlescent response if it remains physically credible and non-RGB;
- exact motion pacing and stage choreography;
- final stage naming and microcopy.

These should be resolved by composition, narrative clarity and visual quality, not by majority vote among AI reviews.

## 6. Advice explicitly treated as low-value / rejected unless a later visual test proves otherwise

- returning to 2.5D/C-shape;

- mandatory sound design/autoplay audio;

## 7. Open decisions intentionally remain open until owner review

Do not silently lock these merely because a prototype chose one answer:

- final stage naming: `TRUST / INQUIRY / RESPONSE / RESULT` versus a stronger variant such as `TRUST / INQUIRY / RESPONSE / FOLLOW-THROUGH` or another owner-approved refinement;
- exact microcopy under each stage;
- exact Core geometry language;
- exact card/plate arrangement;
- exact signal/input-process-output visualization;
- exact amount of warm reflection / pearlescent behavior;
- exact choreography duration and whether all four stages auto-play once or use another restrained presentation.

## 8. Review workflow after the active prototype finishes

When the current prototype is complete:

1. Inspect it as a whole before changing details.
2. Compare it against the synthesis and owner intent.
3. Identify:
   - KEEP — elements that genuinely work;
   - REMOVE — obvious noise/generic treatment;
   - CHANGE — promising but underdeveloped ideas;
   - ADD — missing narrative/material/compositional elements.
4. Preserve good unexpected ideas from the prototype even if they were not explicitly predicted by the synthesis.
5. Do not restart concept selection.
6. Produce the next corrective build/spec only after owner review.
