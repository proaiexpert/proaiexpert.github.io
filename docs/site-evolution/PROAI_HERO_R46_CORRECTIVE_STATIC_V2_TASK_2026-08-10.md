- partial disappearance of dark graphite planes;

- weakened edge integrity;
- locally ragged or dissolved metal boundaries;
- reduced sense of solid expensive material.

The object must never look old, worn, dirty, scratched, erased or partially transparent.

The required order should visually read as:

`FOREGROUND METAL → DEEP INTERNAL CHAMBER → CYAN INTELLIGENCE VOLUME → COLLECTOR/EMISSION ZONE → SHORT EXTERNAL OUTPUT → RAIL`

V1 does not yet make this hierarchy strong enough.

### P1.1 — internal cyan has more glow but not enough embedded volume

V1 adds cyan bloom, but additional glow alone is not the goal.

The viewer must feel that cyan energy occupies internal depth planes and is partially hidden by front structure.

### P1.2 — effects must be softer and more expensive

The final static needs softer, more controlled optical behavior:

- refined bloom rather than bright graphic glow;
- subtle light spill;
- refined metal response;
- restrained contact/floor response;
- no harsh neon or gaming aesthetic.
