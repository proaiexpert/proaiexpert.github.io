## 3. Earlier multi-review audit synthesis — retained as diagnostic history

Recovered project context from the broader audit period identified recurring weaknesses that motivated the Homepage V2 program:

- positioning could read too broad before the offer became concrete;
- service pages sometimes explained the philosophy more clearly than the Homepage explained the first purchasable/usable scope;
- proof depth was thinner for AI/automation than for website work;
- some EN copy could become over-authored instead of commercially direct;
- production history had accumulated patches/version drift and needed stronger source-of-truth discipline.

These are **historical drivers**, not automatically current defects. Before treating any as a live problem, verify the current source/live state.

The review discipline that emerged was:

1. **Positioning Precision**
2. **Service Clarity**
3. **Proof Depth**
4. **Production Integrity**

Premium quality was defined as commercial credibility + editorial control + real evidence + production discipline, not effects alone.

## 4. Accepted strategic logic retained across the site

Long-running accepted direction:

- **AI = front door** — makes the business feel modern, responsive and operationally capable.
- **Systems / automation = core value** — routing, context, workflows, intake, communication support; not “AI for AI's sake.”
- **Websites / branding = trust amplifier** — digital presentation must confirm business quality rather than create doubt.
- **Premium restraint over loud marketing** — no aggressive funnels, popups, cheap agency language, excessive neon or hype.
- **Trust/proof before conversion pressure** — strengthen credibility and clarity first, then improve inquiry flow.
- **Service-business focus** — practical systems for service SMBs, not generic technology theater.
