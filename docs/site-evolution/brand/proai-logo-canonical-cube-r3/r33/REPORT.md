# ProAI Logo R3.3 — Final Signature Identity Polish

R3.3 is a final optical refinement of the accepted R3.2 architecture. It does not create a new symbol, new logo system, or production rollout.

## Identity lock

- Exact canonical physical ProAI Cube on the left.
- Exact wordmark `ProAI Expert` on the right.
- One horizontal row.
- Obsidian master environment.
- Static master plus bounded living digital signature.

## Optical integration

R3.3 treats Cube and wordmark as one signature unit. The master spacing is reduced from the R3.2 28px Cube-to-wordmark gap to 24px, while the Cube receives a small optical vertical correction rather than relying on mathematical center alignment. Header presentation moves from a 28px Cube frame to 30px while retaining a 20px wordmark, improving physical recognition without turning the Cube into an illustration.

The R3.2 Header CSS brightness/contrast/saturation compensation is removed. Small-scale legibility is instead handled through optical size, crop, home portrait, and the existing physically coherent `premiumHybrid` material response.

## Typography

Instrument Sans Variable remains the identity family. The candidate final weight is 680, deliberately between R3.2's 700 and a lighter fashion-like treatment. `ProAI` and `Expert` are tuned independently. The `o→A` and `A→I` joins receive tiny local optical corrections so `ProAI` reads as one proprietary word while `AI` remains unmistakably uppercase.

The wordmark remains primarily typographic: only a restrained Pearl/Silver tonal response is used, with no shimmer, glow, shadow, chromatic accent, or animated text.

## Cube and motion

Geometry and GLB are unchanged. The candidate home portrait is a narrow refinement around the R3.2 presentation neighborhood and will be accepted only after direct Header-scale visual review. The living signature uses two low-rate, non-matching oscillations around home instead of a broad 360° path, preventing spinner behavior and avoiding a perceptible short loop.

## QA intent

R3.3 must pass direct visual review at master, desktop Header, 390px mobile, narrow mobile, grayscale, and blur/squint conditions. The public owner URL is not considered valid until a real browser loads CSS, JS, GLB, renders the Cube and wordmark, and observes motion.
