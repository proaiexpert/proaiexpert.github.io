# ProAI Logo R3.3 — Final Signature Identity Polish

R3.3 is the final optical refinement of the accepted R3.2 architecture. It does not create a new symbol, alternative logo system, or production rollout.

## Identity lock

- Exact canonical physical ProAI Cube on the left.
- Exact wordmark `ProAI Expert` on the right.
- One horizontal row.
- Obsidian master environment.
- Static master plus bounded living digital signature.

## Final optical integration

R3.3 treats Cube and wordmark as one signature unit. The master Cube-to-wordmark frame gap is reduced from R3.2's 28px to 24px. The Cube receives a small optical vertical correction (`-1.5px` master / `-0.5px` Header) rather than relying on mathematical center alignment. Header presentation moves from a 28px Cube frame to 30px while retaining the 20px wordmark, improving immediate physical recognition without turning the Cube into an illustration.

The R3.2 Header CSS `brightness/contrast/saturate` compensation is removed completely. Small-scale legibility is handled through optical sizing, crop, home portrait, and the existing physically coherent `premiumHybrid` Three.js render.

## Typography

Instrument Sans Variable remains the identity family. Direct 660 / 680 / 700 visual comparison selected **680**: 660 loses a little authority at Header scale, while 700 retains too much of R3.2's visual heaviness. `ProAI` and `Expert` are tuned independently. Tiny local `o→A` and `A→I` corrections keep `ProAI` reading as one proprietary word while preserving unmistakable uppercase `AI`.

The wordmark remains primarily typographic. ProAI uses restrained warm Pearl (`#F1EFEA → #ECEAE5`); Expert uses slightly cooler engineered Silver (`#CDD1D0 → #C4C8C9`). There is no shimmer, glow, shadow, chromatic accent, or text motion.

## Canonical home portrait

A narrow visual search around the successful R3.2 neighborhood selected **49.6s** of the frozen canonical presentation path:

- face: `+X`
- visibility dot: `0.8190523276185581`
- yaw: `288.8215018750006°`
- pitch: `2.73531610687214°`
- roll: `-1.481584561942659°`
- camera FOV: `31°`

49.0s becomes too frontal; 49.9–50.2s increase side emphasis. 49.6s gives the strongest logo portrait balance: dominant primary plane, explicit secondary depth plane, restrained top-plane confirmation, and a stable silhouette beside the wordmark.

## Cube lookdev

The exact existing GLB and geometry are unchanged. The canonical `premiumHybrid` material family remains selected. The primary plane carries Graphite/Gunmetal under a broad studio key; the secondary plane falls darker through Gunmetal/Black Chrome; the top carries a controlled Pearl/Silver catch; recesses remain Smoked Core near-black. Physical bevels provide selective specular construction cues without outlines or glow.

## Living signature

The digital signature remains a bounded portrait, not a 360° viewer. Two low-rate non-matching oscillations keep movement continuous around the static home portrait without a short visible loop. Approximate yaw velocity at the home crossing is ~3.48°/s. Wordmark motion, slice choreography and semantic face are disabled.
