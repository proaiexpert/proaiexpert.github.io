## Strategic separation

**STATIC MASTER IDENTITY FIRST. DIGITAL MOTION COMPANION SECOND.**

The static mark/wordmark lockup must work completely frozen. The motion companion exists only as an optional digital extension. No concept depends on animation for recognition.

## Concept A — Signature Corner

A premium open-core signature object built from three machined members. It recalls the spatial logic and material behavior of the ProAI Cube, but does not close into a stock isometric cube. The missing upper-right member is intentional: it creates a repeatable asymmetric signature cut and gives the mark an ownable silhouette. Pearl/Silver is limited to the upper plane and one Ice-biased optical edge response.

Strengths: strongest relationship to the Cube memory asset; best combination of large-form presence and 28–32px silhouette; natural motion-companion behavior; clean lockup with `ProAI Expert`.

Potential weakness: 16px must collapse material nuance into simple tonal separation; no bevel micro-detail should survive at that size.

## Concept B — Engineered Monolith

A reduced, front-facing product mark rather than a cube. A chamfered monolith carries an offset precision core, giving it the strongest stamp/print behavior of the set. It borrows the Cube's machined material language without borrowing its spatial silhouette.

Strengths: most logo-native; strongest monochrome and print logic; cleanest pure reduction; least likely to be mistaken for a crypto/SaaS cube.

Potential weakness: the conceptual relationship to the living ProAI Cube is weaker than A and therefore requires the wider brand system to make the connection.

## Concept C — Connected System

Three engineered links share one central joint and create a continuous, physically plausible system path. It suggests integration/orchestration without using nodes, circuits, a generic hexagon or a network diagram. The structure is deliberately mechanical rather than symbolic-tech decorative.

Strengths: strongest system/integration story; distinctive at medium and large sizes; good digital brand language.

Potential weakness: densest of the three; the joint logic is the first thing to collapse near 28px and therefore it is the weakest primary Header candidate.

## Independent recommendation

**A — Signature Corner.**

Ranked reasoning:

1. **A** — best overall: it preserves Cube brand memory, introduces an ownable asymmetric silhouette and remains viable in the current 28–32px Header envelope.
2. **B** — strongest alternative: more reduction-native and excellent for print, but less connected to the Cube memory asset.
3. **C** — strategically coherent but denser; better as a secondary systems motif than as the primary identity.

## Wordmark direction

Exact naming remains `ProAI Expert`. R2 uses a quiet weight hierarchy rather than color shouting: `ProAI` medium/semibold, `Expert` same family at a lighter weight/value. No bright cyan `EXPERT`, neon outline, sci-fi casing or forced all-caps dependency is introduced.

Final production typeface optical tuning is intentionally deferred until the owner selects a mark direction. The boards use Inter/system fallback to test proportion, not to claim a finished custom wordmark drawing.

## Dark-background suitability

**PASS.** All three are designed first for Obsidian / Graphite fields. A is strongest because the open core and Pearl plane stay legible without glow.

## Light-background sanity

**PASS with production inversion tuning required.** The neutral black-chrome body and tonal top planes survive on Pearl/Off-white. The review board deliberately avoids a separate unrelated inverse icon; final production assets should tune edge values rather than change geometry.

## Header suitability

- A: **PASS** — strongest 30–32px identity memory.
- B: **PASS** — calmest and easiest to reduce.
- C: **QUALIFIED** — viable at 32px but the structural story gets dense at 28px.

The review uses the current canonical 85px legacy Header envelope only as a compatibility constraint. It does not change the Header implementation.

## Small-size viability

- A: **PASS** at 28–32px; future 16px favicon requires simplified three-tone rendering.
- B: **PASS** at 28–32px and strongest future 16px reduction.
- C: **QUALIFIED** at 28px; stronger at 32px+.

## Motion companion candidate

**Concept A.**

Motion behavior: 18-second quiet orientation drift, approximately ±4° around a stable home angle. No full rotation, no loading-spinner read, no bright glow, no bounce. A localized Pearl/Ice edge response may move once per loop at low intensity. `prefers-reduced-motion` resolves to the complete static home angle.
