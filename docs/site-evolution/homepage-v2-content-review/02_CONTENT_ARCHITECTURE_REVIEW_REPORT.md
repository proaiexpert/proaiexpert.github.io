## 1. Executive verdict

The proposed Homepage V2 Content Architecture successfully converts the approved strategy into a coherent ten-block commercial journey.

The central architecture is sound:

- the audience is clearly U.S. service businesses;
- the Hero states recognizable business outcomes without becoming a service catalogue;
- AI Systems & Automation remains first in positioning;
- Premium Websites & Branding remains the stronger visible proof path;
- the before-inquiry / after-inquiry thesis is understandable without animation;
- Financial Stream appears early enough to establish real proof;
- Ways to Start follows proof at the correct conversion moment;
- Process and Founder reduce delivery risk without interrupting the initial argument;
- Alina Horb and Local Repair Pro have explicit evidence boundaries;
- the final Private Review is bounded and does not introduce another service pitch;
- the responsive content order is viable without a wide-diagram dependency;
- the future Jekyll ownership model is fundamentally appropriate.

No evidence supports rejecting or substantially reconstructing the ten-block architecture.

However, the document is not yet ready to become the accepted source for low-fidelity mapping and future EN/RU YAML because several specific issues remain:

1. the required `meta` data owner is named but its EN/RU content contract is absent;
2. secondary/deep CTA identifiers are not fully reconciled with the approved Production Specification and implementation parity requirements;
3. the Ways to Start data shape is ambiguous for the combined situation, which requires two service links while the stated schema names one `service_link`;
4. one Financial Stream sentence uses an unnecessarily absolute first-call formulation;
5. several RU status labels and sentences are grammatically weak, commercially awkward, or less precise than their EN equivalents;
6. the long RU Hero opening must remain provisional until it is demonstrated at 320 px and short landscape.

These are bounded, fixable corrections. They do not invalidate the page strategy, block order, proof hierarchy, conversion model, or visual direction.

**Verdict: `TARGETED CORRECTION`.**

## 3. Commercial clarity review

### Audience and company understanding

**PASS.**

The Hero identifies the audience in the eyebrow and communicates three practical business outcomes in the H1:

- trust;
- inquiry handling;
- reduced manual work.

The supporting copy then explains the company model through premium websites, practical automation and human-reviewed AI. A service-business owner can understand what ProAI Expert improves without reading a technical service matrix.

**PASS.**

`Build trust. Handle inquiries. Reduce manual work.` is specific enough to orient a buyer while remaining broader than a list of deliverables. It matches the approved central message and does not depend on AI novelty language.

**PASS.**

The architecture explains why websites and automation belong together through a clear division:

- the website shapes understanding, proof, trust and action before contact;
- automation protects context, ownership, response and follow-up after contact.

This is the strongest differentiating argument in the document and is presented before service-direction selection.

### AI emphasis and claims

**PASS with one copy correction.**

AI appears first in the two-direction reading order and remains the strategic growth direction. The copy consistently preserves human responsibility and avoids autonomous-agent claims.

The document uses intended-effect language rather than measured-outcome language. The only material commercial-precision correction is the absolute first-call phrase in the Financial Stream body, addressed in Section 13.

### Generic agency language

**PASS.**

The document avoids generic claims such as “transform your business,” “10x growth,” “cutting-edge solutions,” unsupported ROI, guaranteed rankings, or autonomous operation.
