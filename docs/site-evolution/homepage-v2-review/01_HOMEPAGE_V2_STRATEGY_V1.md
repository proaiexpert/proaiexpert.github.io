## 1. Executive decision

The current homepage is visually competent and technically mature, but it presents a broad catalogue of capabilities more strongly than a clear commercial path.

Homepage V2 must not be a cosmetic restyle. It must rebuild the body narrative around:

1. recognizable business problems;
2. two clear service directions;
3. one connected customer journey;
4. early real proof;
5. one low-friction primary action;
6. premium proof-led composition;
7. deliberate EN/RU and responsive parity.

The unifying business idea is:

> ProAI Expert builds the digital systems that help service businesses earn trust, handle inquiries, and operate with less manual work.

The homepage must explain both sides of the customer journey:

- **Before the inquiry:** positioning, branding, website, content, trust and a clear contact path.
- **After the inquiry:** intake, acknowledgement, notification, routing, logging, follow-up and human-reviewed automation.

This is what differentiates ProAI Expert from a conventional web studio, a generic AI agency and a strategy-only consultant.

## 2. Locked strategic decisions

### 2.1 Audience

Primary audience:

> Service businesses across the United States.

Washington State is a geographic advantage, founder location and acquisition priority, not a hard service limitation.

Do not divide the homepage into separate home-service, professional-service, beauty or wellness experiences.

### 2.2 Two main directions

The homepage presents only two top-level disciplines:

1. **AI Systems & Automation**
2. **Premium Websites & Branding**

Branding, consulting, offer clarity, SEO, content, lead response, integrations and ongoing optimization are supporting capabilities inside these two disciplines.

### 2.3 Internal commercial emphasis

Internal direction:

- approximately 60% AI Systems & Automation;
- approximately 40% Premium Websites & Branding.

Do not display these percentages publicly or translate them mechanically into unequal card sizes.

Operating principle:

> AI first in positioning. Websites first in visible proof.

AI and automation remain the long-term strategic direction. Websites remain the clearest current market entry and strongest visual proof.

### 2.4 Founder role

The brand remains studio-first and founder-supported.

Ihor Horb appears as:

- Founder;
- Strategy & Systems Architecture;
- the person personally accountable for architecture and quality.

The homepage must not become a founder-centric personal landing page.

### 2.5 Pricing

Do not show prices on the homepage.

The homepage may explain why project scope varies, but detailed ranges belong on service or offer pages after separate approval.

### 2.6 Header and Footer

Header System and Footer System are locked.

Homepage V2 must integrate with them without redesigning their architecture, styling or behavior.

## 3. Commercial positioning

### 3.1 Primary business problem

A service business may be stronger than its current digital system.

The problem occurs in two places.

#### Before the inquiry

- weak or outdated presentation;
- unclear offer;
- insufficient trust;
- poor mobile experience;
- unclear contact path;
- inconsistent branding;
- content that does not help the visitor decide.

#### After the inquiry

- incomplete requests;
- missed notifications;
- slow or inconsistent follow-up;
- fragmented information;
- repetitive manual work;
- no clear ownership;
- disconnected tools.

### 3.2 Primary outcome

ProAI Expert helps create:

- stronger trust;
- clearer customer understanding;
- a better inquiry path;
- more structured intake;
- faster and more controlled response;
- less repetitive manual work;
- a more professional digital operating system.

### 3.3 Differentiation

#### Not only a web studio

ProAI Expert connects website work to positioning, inquiry architecture, operations, automation and ongoing improvement.

#### Not a generic AI agency

ProAI Expert does not lead with agents, models or AI spectacle. It sells controlled improvements to real business processes with clear human ownership.

#### Not strategy-only consulting

ProAI Expert connects diagnosis, strategy, design, implementation, integration, QA and ongoing optimization.

## 4. Terminology and service framing

### 4.1 Lead response system

A lead response system may include:

- a structured inquiry form;
- automatic acknowledgement;
- staff notification;
- routing;
- lead logging;
- reminders;
- email or SMS response;
- human-reviewed AI drafts;
- CRM or spreadsheet connection;
- failure notifications.

Do not use `Lead Response Systems` as the homepage's main public service label. Explain these capabilities inside **AI Systems & Automation** using plain business language.

### 4.2 Strategic diagnostic

A strategic diagnostic is a professional review of the website, positioning, inquiry path, operational process and automation opportunity.

It is not a third top-level service. It is the beginning of work and part of the primary CTA.

### 4.3 Offer clarity

Offer clarity means making clear:

- who the business helps;
- what problem it solves;
- what the client receives;
- how it differs;
- why it can be trusted;
- what action the visitor should take.

Offer clarity belongs inside **Premium Websites & Branding**.

## 13. Premium visual direction

Strategic direction:

> Preserve the ProAI dark technological identity, but rebuild the page with premium editorial rhythm and proof-led surfaces.

### Preserve

- dark ProAI foundation;
- cyan accent;
- strong typography;
- system-oriented identity;
- technological precision;
- current Header and Footer.

### Change

- reduce generic AI glow;
- remove decorative large 3D spectacle;
- reduce identical black sections;
- remove unnecessary micro-nodes and logo walls;
- use real screenshots and proof;
- introduce editorial rhythm and intentional surface changes;
- use project-specific colors where proof requires them;
- create visual pauses and stronger reading hierarchy.

### Surface roles

1. Opening — near-black, restrained system visualization.
2. Problem — graphite/editorial contrast.
3. Connected System — technical flow surface.
4. Directions — distinct AI and website territories.
5. Flagship Proof — Financial Stream project world.
6. Human — calmer founder surface.
7. Insights — editorial and lower density.
8. Closing — canonical ProAI dark transition into Footer.

### Images

Prefer real project screenshots, interface details, process diagrams and a real founder portrait.

Avoid generic robots, humanoid heads, random neural networks, stock handshakes, fake dashboards and decorative device mockups without evidence value.

### Motion

Allow restrained reveals, state changes, path activation and proof transitions.

Do not use scroll-jacking, particles, cursor trails, typing effects, autoplay storytelling, continuous parallax or content-dependent animation.

## 14. Responsive strategy

### Desktop

Use full editorial rhythm, meaningful asymmetry and large proof without excessively wide text.

### Laptop

Reduce decorative scale, prevent horizontal overflow and retain readable proof.

### Tablet

Recompose rather than shrink. Stack the two directions when required. Keep CTA near its message and simplify the signature system.

### Phone portrait

Required order:

1. audience;
2. outcome;
3. supporting statement;
4. primary CTA;
5. secondary CTA;
6. proof or system visual.

No desktop miniatures, tiny labels or horizontal overflow. Use focused screenshot crops.

Required checks: 430, 390, 375, 360 and 320 px.

### Phone landscape

Design for low height. Avoid an oversized Hero, keep primary action reachable, simplify diagrams and prevent Header/sticky collisions.
