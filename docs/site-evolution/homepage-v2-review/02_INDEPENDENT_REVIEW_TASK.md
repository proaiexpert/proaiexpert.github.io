## Review requirements

### Commercial clarity

Determine whether a visitor can understand within 5–10 seconds:

- who ProAI Expert serves;
- what problems it solves;
- why websites and automation belong together;
- which first step is available;
- why the company is different from a normal web studio and generic AI agency.

### Narrative

Review the proposed order from Hero through final conversion.

Identify:

- missing logical steps;
- duplicated arguments;
- proof appearing too early or too late;
- sections with unclear commercial function;
- excessive length or service-catalogue risk.

### Service architecture

Check whether two top-level directions are sufficient and whether supporting capabilities are correctly nested.

Pay particular attention to whether AI Systems & Automation remains understandable to a nontechnical business owner.

### CTA architecture

Check:

- clarity of `Request a Private Review`;
- risk that it implies a large free audit;
- consistency of section CTAs;
- path to Contact;
- context preservation;
- whether `View Client Work` is the correct secondary action.

### Proof architecture

Check:

- Financial Stream placement and evidence boundaries;
- Alina related-party disclosure requirements;
- Local Repair concept status;
- duplication between flagship proof and Selected Work;
- whether current proof is sufficient for the proposed claims.

### Visual strategy

Evaluate whether the proposed direction:

- preserves recognizable ProAI identity;
- moves far enough away from generic AI spectacle;
- supports a premium editorial rhythm;
- uses proof more strongly than decoration;
- can integrate with the locked Header and Footer;
- contains a meaningful homepage signature system.

### Responsive strategy

Evaluate:

- desktop;
- laptop;
- tablet;
- phone portrait;
- phone landscape;
- 320 px narrow mobile.

Identify desktop-only ideas, difficult diagrams, likely overflow or density risks, and sections requiring substantial recomposition.

### EN/RU parity

Check:

- business-intent parity;
- CTA parity;
- proof parity;
- likely RU line-expansion problems;
- sections requiring independent Russian composition;
- any terminology that is weak or unnatural in either language.

## Required verdict
