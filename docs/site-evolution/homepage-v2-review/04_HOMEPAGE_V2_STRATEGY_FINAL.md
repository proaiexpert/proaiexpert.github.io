# 1. EXECUTIVE DECISION

## 1.1 Commercial role of the homepage

The Homepage is the primary commercial orientation page for ProAI Expert.

It must:

1. identify the intended audience;
2. name the business problems ProAI Expert addresses;
3. explain why websites and automation belong in one system;
4. present two clear service directions;
5. establish credibility through verified work;
6. give a low-friction first step;
7. route visitors into relevant services, cases, insights and contact context.

The Homepage is not:

- an exhaustive service catalogue;
- a technical AI architecture page;
- a portfolio archive;
- a founder biography;
- a pricing page;
- a long-form methodology page.

## 1.2 Central strategic idea

ProAI Expert improves both sides of the customer journey:

### Before the inquiry

- positioning;
- brand and presentation;
- website clarity;
- trust;
- service understanding;
- the path to contact.

### After the inquiry

- context capture;
- acknowledgement;
- notification;
- routing;
- response support;
- follow-up;
- operational improvement.

This creates one defensible commercial thesis:

> **ProAI Expert builds the digital systems that help service businesses earn trust, handle inquiries and operate with less manual work.**

## 1.3 Two top-level directions

The Homepage presents only:

1. **AI Systems & Automation**
2. **Premium Websites & Branding**

Strategy, consulting, localization, content, offer clarity, integrations, search architecture and ongoing improvement remain supporting capabilities within these two directions.

## 1.4 Internal emphasis

Internal planning may retain an approximate 60/40 strategic emphasis:

- AI Systems & Automation: approximately 60%;
- Premium Websites & Branding: approximately 40%.

The percentages are never shown publicly.

The public rule is:

> **AI first in positioning. Websites first in visible proof.**

## 1.5 Audience

Primary audience:

> **Service businesses across the United States.**

Washington is used as:

- founder location;
- current acquisition priority;
- local-market credibility;
- a practical relationship advantage.

Washington is not presented as a service boundary.

Recommended credibility line:

**EN:** `Washington-based. Working with service businesses across the U.S.`  
**RU:** `Базируемся в штате Вашингтон. Работаем с сервисным бизнесом по всей территории США.`

# 3. HOMEPAGE POSITIONING

## 3.1 Primary business problem

The business may be stronger than its current digital system.

Typical breakdowns appear in three supported categories:

### Trust friction

- the business looks weaker online than it is;
- services are difficult to understand;
- proof is hard to find;
- the mobile experience creates doubt;
- branding and presentation are inconsistent.

### Inquiry friction

- visitors do not know what to submit;
- forms collect too little or too much information;
- the path to contact is unclear;
- calendar-first flows ask for commitment before context;
- inquiries arrive through disconnected channels.

### Operational friction

- staff repeat the same administrative work;
- notifications and handoffs depend on memory;
- context is lost between tools or people;
- follow-up is inconsistent;
- exceptions require human judgement but the process does not make that clear.

Do not use `revenue friction` as a Homepage category.

## 3.2 Intended business change

Homepage copy may state that ProAI systems are designed to support:

- stronger trust before contact;
- clearer requests;
- more consistent response handling;
- clearer handoffs;
- fewer avoidable manual steps;
- better operational visibility.

Homepage copy must not state unverified improvements in:

- revenue;
- ROI;
- lead volume;
- lead quality;
- conversion rate;
- ranking performance;
- response time;
- customer acquisition cost.

## 3.3 Differentiation

### Not a conventional web studio

ProAI Expert connects website presentation with:

- positioning;
- inquiry architecture;
- operational response;
- integrations;
- ongoing improvement.

### Not a generic AI agency

ProAI Expert does not lead with:

- agents;
- models;
- orchestration;
- autonomous operation;
- dashboards;
- technical spectacle.

It leads with a defined business process and clear human boundaries.

### Not a strategy-only consultancy

ProAI Expert connects:

- analysis;
- architecture;
- design;
- implementation;
- integration;
- verification;
- controlled improvement.
