# Concept name

## THE OPERATIONAL REGISTER — SIGNAL → CONTROL

## Page role

Convert AI curiosity into confidence that ProAI can design a controlled operating layer around real service-business work.

The page must answer:

- what ProAI actually builds;
- where AI helps;
- where normal automation or process change is better;
- how intake, context, routing, follow-up and escalation connect;
- where human judgment remains mandatory;
- what a safe first implementation phase looks like.

## One-sentence promise

**Build the controlled layer between incoming signal and human action—using AI only where it earns its place.**

RU should be written natively around the same meaning, not translated word-for-word.

## Signature visual idea

A business inquiry initially arrives as **misregistered operational fragments**: source, service type, urgency, history, owner, missing context, next action.

During the first major scroll moment, those fragments **register into one controlled plane** and resolve into five functional states:

`CAPTURE → CONTEXT → ROUTE → HUMAN CHECK → ACTION`

This is not a node diagram, flowchart, dashboard, moving rail or 3D sculpture.

The visual metaphor is **registration / alignment**: scattered information becomes a controlled operating state.

The sequence plays once, resolves, and becomes still.

## Hero

### Composition

Desktop:

- canonical Header;
- dark graphite/blued-steel field;
- large editorial H1 and explanatory lead on the left ~5–6 columns;
- one restrained primary CTA + proof/deeper secondary link;
- on the right / across the composition, 5–7 thin information fragments appear slightly misregistered on material planes;
- no rounded card pile;
- no fake software chrome;
- no robot/brain/AI symbol;
- no permanent ambient animation.

### Content role

Hero copy must establish:

1. category: AI systems + automation;
2. business mechanism: intake/context/routing/workflows;
3. judgment: not everything should use AI;
4. human control.

Do not lead with tool names.

## First scroll moment

The fragments move by a small, physically plausible distance and align onto one register surface.

As alignment completes, labels resolve:

- CAPTURE
- CONTEXT
- ROUTE
- HUMAN CHECK
- ACTION

A single active state can briefly receive the controlled indigo accent.

Duration target: ~900–1100ms total signature assembly, tied to scroll progress or triggered once near entry. After completion, the surface is still.

Reduced motion: show the final aligned state immediately with a short opacity transition.

## Content chapters

### 01 — Where operational complexity is actually lost

Use the strongest existing problem logic:

- incoming inquiries become manual triage;
- context is fragmented;
- handoffs depend on memory;
- repeated admin work consumes attention;
- poor process visibility hides failure points.

Present as one narrative field with a few anchored examples, not three equal problem cards.

### 02 — What ProAI builds

Organize capabilities by business function, not tool category:

- intake / qualification;
- routing / task movement;
- context / knowledge access;
- communication support;
- follow-up / recurring actions;
- reporting / escalation.

Use an editorial decision architecture rather than a SaaS feature matrix.

### 03 — The Operational Register

The signature model becomes explanatory:

- what information enters;
- what context is added;
- what rules or AI interpretation occur;
- where an exception requires a human;
- what downstream action is permitted.

Keep this conceptual and honest; do not mimic a proprietary product UI that does not exist.

### 04 — AI vs automation vs process change

Promote this to a major chapter.

Three decision conditions:

1. **Use normal automation** when inputs and rules are stable and deterministic.
2. **Use AI** when language/context classification or flexible synthesis creates real value and the risk boundary is understood.
3. **Fix the process first** when ownership, sequence or rules are unclear.

The RU page's current anti-hype logic is a major authority source and should be preserved.

### 05 — Human control / governance

Show concrete boundaries:

- review-required decisions;
- permissioned context;
- escalation;
- auditability/visibility where implementation supports it;
- rollout stages;
- failure/exception handling.

Do not oversell autonomous operation.

### 06 — Implementation protocol

Preserve the current process-first discipline but evolve the presentation:

`AUDIT → ARCHITECTURE → BUILD → CONTROLLED LAUNCH → REFINE`

Each stage should show one decision/output rather than existing as a generic step card.

### 07 — Proof / examples

Current R0 evidence does **not** justify invented AI-client performance metrics.

Therefore R1 uses one of two truthful proof forms:

- a real implementation artifact if repository evidence exists and claim scope is verified; or
- explicitly labeled **reference scenario / system pattern** demonstrating inquiry routing or context assembly.

Never label a hypothetical scenario as a client result.

### 08 — Integrations / implementation context

A quiet text/index treatment can mention supported categories or verified tools where evidence exists.

Do not use:

- giant logo walls;
- infinite marquees;
- tool catalog as core value.

### 09 — Next step

The final CTA asks for the bottleneck, not for a pre-defined AI project.

Suggested EN action:

`Discuss the system`

Support intent:

“Describe the bottleneck. We’ll identify whether AI, automation, or a simpler process change is the right first step.”

RU should be native and equally direct.

## Material identity

### Core

- Obsidian background.
- Graphite / gunmetal planes.
- Cool blued-steel optical edge behavior.
- Pearl for primary type / high-priority evidence.
- Controlled indigo/violet machine accent only for active state/decision.

### Prohibited

- cyan fog;
- purple AI gradients;
- node-map neon;
- terminal green;
- holographic robot imagery;
- particle field;
- another Cube.

## Color logic

The page should be mostly neutral.

Accent means:

- active state;
- selected decision;
- controlled transition;
- exception / human review boundary where appropriate.

Accent does not mean “make this look more AI.”

## Typography

Use local Inter variable.

The technical feeling comes from:

- alignment;
- labels;
- precision;
- hierarchy;
- data/state behavior;

—not from excessive monospace text.

Hero should be large but readable; no cropped macro type.

## Proof logic

Proof hierarchy:

1. verified real implementation artifact;
2. verified architecture / workflow artifact;
3. bounded reference scenario explicitly labeled;
4. methodology / governance rules.

Do not convert structural logic into unsupported outcome claims.

## Motion

Motion budget is intentionally small:

- one Operational Register alignment sequence;
- small state emphasis when reading the model;
- standard chapter reveals;
- restrained fine-pointer response if it helps state interpretation.

No permanent animation loop.

## Pointer

Fine pointer can locally bias a highlight across the register plane by a few pixels or reveal a state label.

Pointer must not:

- distort the whole scene;
- act as a magnetic cursor;
- be required to understand the system.

Touch receives direct state reading through scroll/tap only.

## Mobile signature moment

Mobile begins with one raw inquiry/context block rather than a shrunken desktop field.

As the user scrolls, it resolves vertically through:

`CAPTURE`
`CONTEXT`
`ROUTE`
`HUMAN CHECK`
`ACTION`

Only one or two states are visually dominant at once.

The signature becomes **progressive registration**, not a miniature diagram.

## Mobile content rules

- Opening H1 + lead + one CTA fit into a deliberate first composition; no forced full-viewport trap.
- Proof/scenario appears earlier than on desktop.
- No hover dependency.
- Matrices become labeled sequences.
- Aim for 70–120 words maximum between meaningful visual/heading breaks.
- No sticky chapters on short landscape devices unless fully bypassable.
- Nothing essential is removed; decorative misregistration layers may simplify heavily.

## CTA

Primary: `Discuss the system`.

Secondary near opening: `See how the system works` or equivalent if the destination is a real in-page chapter.

Final CTA explains what ProAI will decide with the prospect.

## Why this can feel like a $100K bespoke page

Because the page's memorable behavior is generated by the service meaning itself:

- scattered operational information becomes controlled state;
- AI/no-AI judgment is a first-class chapter;
- governance is visible rather than buried;
- proof is bounded instead of fabricated;
- motion explains a transition and then stops;
- mobile receives a different narrative implementation;
- the visual system is not a reusable “AI agency” template.

A generic agency could imitate the colors quickly. It could not reproduce the concept convincingly without understanding ProAI's operational logic, evidence constraints, bilingual content and implementation system.
