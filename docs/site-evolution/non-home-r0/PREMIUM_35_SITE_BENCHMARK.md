## Method

Exactly 35 current public sites were opened through live web research. The set is intentionally cross-category:

- 8 premium digital / interactive / brand studios;
- 7 premium technology / AI / product companies;
- 5 architecture / industrial design / engineering brands;
- 5 automotive / luxury / high-craft brands;
- 5 fashion / culture / editorial / museum experiences;
- 5 premium B2B / professional / service-business sites.

No site is treated as a template to copy. The question is whether a pattern is useful for ProAI's service-business positioning, proof, bilingual editorial system and implementation constraints.

## 1. Work & Co

- **URL:** https://work.co/
- **CATEGORY:** Digital product / design / technology studio
- **WHY INCLUDED:** Exceptional economy: proposition, third-party authority and recent work arrive quickly without a feature catalog.
- **FIRST-IMPRESSION AUTHORITY:** Very high. “We solve complex problems through design & technology” is immediately followed by recognizable trust evidence.
- **TYPOGRAPHY:** Restrained, editorial, proposition-led; type carries hierarchy instead of decoration.
- **SPATIAL COMPOSITION:** Large fields and decisive work modules rather than dense card walls.
- **MATERIAL / COLOR:** Neutral brand surface lets work provide chromatic variation.

## Benchmark conclusion

The strongest cross-category signal is **not dark metal, 3D, oversized type or constant motion**.

Across the useful references, premium authority comes from:

1. one clear proposition;
2. early proof or evidence of competence;
3. authored hierarchy rather than uniform card grids;
4. real work, products, projects, research or institutional evidence;
5. motion that reveals state or craft, followed by stillness;
6. material/color restraint with deliberate variation;
7. mobile content priority;
8. clear commercial next actions without funnel aggression.

For ProAI specifically, the benchmark argues for **more editorial space and more light material contrast on Websites & Branding**, while AI Systems can remain darker/cooler but must avoid dashboards, terminal UI, node diagrams and generic “AI glow.”
