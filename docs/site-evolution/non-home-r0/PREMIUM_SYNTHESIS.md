# ProAI Expert — Premium Benchmark Synthesis R0

Status: planning authority for non-home redesign  
Date: 2026-08-21

## Central synthesis

The benchmark does **not** support a louder ProAI site. It supports a more edited one.

The strongest references earn premium authority through a combination of proposition clarity, authored composition, evidence, disciplined material behavior and confidence to let the page settle. This argues against extending the old ProAI cyan/dark-panel vocabulary simply with better animation.

## Top 10 principles to adopt

1. **One dominant proposition per page.** The visitor should know the page's job before encountering capability detail.
2. **Put proof close to the claim.** Do not make visitors cross half the page before seeing why a statement is credible.
3. **Use editorial/asymmetric composition as the default; containment only when content truly needs a container.**
4. **Build fewer, more consequential chapters.** Avoid ten visually equivalent sections.
5. **One signature interaction derived from the commercial meaning of the page.**
6. **Use density, whitespace, light/dark material and scale to create transitions before reaching for effects.**
7. **Typography is structural infrastructure.** Control measure, hierarchy, line breaks and label roles.
8. **Long pages need orientation.** Use chapter labels, progress/context or clear transitions—not persistent dashboard UI.
9. **Motion follows `arrive → resolve → settle → breathe`.**
10. **Mobile is recomposed around the decision sequence, not stacked from desktop.**

## Top 10 patterns to reject

1. Generic card grids as default section architecture.
2. Dark-glass panel walls with 20–30px radii everywhere.
3. SaaS dashboards, terminal UI or fake operator screens to make AI look technical.
4. Required horizontal drag or hover to reveal essential information.
5. Giant macro type that sacrifices comprehension.
6. Infinite logo/client marquees.
7. Scroll-jacking or mandatory pinned storytelling.
8. Constant loops, particles, glows or ambient “AI energy.”
9. Anonymous abstract 3D sculptures used only as premium signaling.
10. Framer/Awwwards imitation: oversized headings + floating cards + gradient orb + reveal blur without a business-specific idea.

## Top 5 premium motion principles

1. **State before spectacle:** movement must show state, sequence, priority, cause/effect or physical/material behavior.
2. **Finite choreography:** signature sequences occur once, then stop. No permanent visual agitation.
3. **Duration hierarchy:** micro-interaction ~180–320 ms; chapter reveal ~500–800 ms; major signature assembly ~900–1200 ms maximum unless content genuinely requires longer.
4. **Input-aware behavior:** pointer-only enhancements run only on fine/hover devices; touch receives direct controls and scroll/tap equivalents.
5. **Reduced motion is a first-class mode:** remove transforms and long interpolation while preserving every semantic state.

## Top 5 mobile principles

1. Recompose the opening around one proposition + one signature moment; do not shrink the desktop hero.
2. Show proof earlier because mobile users have less visible context and less patience for abstract setup.
3. Replace hover with tap, scroll position, direct disclosure or no interaction at all.
4. Transform complex matrices into labeled row sequences or deliberate horizontal tables only where two-dimensional comparison is essential.
5. Use 44px+ touch targets, 16px+ body text, controlled copy chunks and no horizontal overflow at 320px.

## Top 5 proof / authority principles

1. **Provenance is part of the design:** identify client/project/status/source/date where relevant.
2. **Dated evidence stays dated:** metrics need source, capture period and limitations.
3. **Status honesty increases authority:** real live client, real website, demo, concept and in-development work must not be visually presented as equivalent.
4. **Artifacts outrank adjectives:** screenshots, architecture, decisions, implementation records and source material are stronger than “premium,” “effective” or “high-converting.”
5. **Limitations belong adjacent to metrics:** do not bury claim boundaries in a legal footnote.

## Top 5 typography principles

1. Keep local Inter variable as the system baseline; differentiation comes from setting, scale and composition, not adding fonts casually.
2. Use fluid but bounded display scales; mobile titles remain large but readable rather than cropped.
3. Body measure should generally remain ~62–72ch; critical explanatory copy should not become ultra-wide.
4. Negative tracking belongs to large display type only; microtype should prioritize readability over style.
5. EN and RU need independent line breaking, copy length and vertical rhythm while preserving semantic parity.

## Top 5 commercial / conversion principles

1. One primary action per page family, expressed contextually (`Discuss the system`, `Discuss your website`, `Start a conversation`).
2. A secondary action should usually lead to proof or deeper understanding, not another funnel step.
3. Explain what happens after contact; reduce uncertainty rather than manufacturing urgency.
4. Place CTA after meaningful understanding/proof; do not repeat conversion blocks every viewport.
5. Keep contact low friction and human: no mandatory calendar, chat widget, account or giant qualification form by default.

## Specific decisions for ProAI

### 1. Do not make every inner page dark metal

The benchmark shows that restrained light editorial planes can increase authority. **Websites & Branding should use materially more pearl/silver space than AI Systems.** This creates sibling differentiation and prevents ProAI from becoming a monochrome “dark tech” site.

### 2. Do not use another dashboard to explain AI

Linear is useful because its UI is its product. ProAI's product is judgment + integration + implementation. A fake interface would misrepresent the service and move the brand toward SaaS theater.

### 3. Use real client proof as the main visual richness for Websites & Branding

The strongest architecture/luxury references let the work carry the page. Financial Stream should do more visual work than abstract website mockup decoration.

### 4. The inner Footer should be a derivative, not a repeated Homepage signature

Homepage Footer R2 has a distinct material/pointer behavior. Repeating the same signature after every long service/article/case page would dilute both the footer and page-specific choreography. Inner pages should reuse the brand/utility/CTA logic with a calmer static or one-time material state.

### 5. Insights should behave like curated intelligence

The museum/editorial references support taxonomy, relationships, provenance and discovery. ProAI should not rebuild Insights as a prettier blog grid.

## Owner decision encoded by R0

**Default direction:** restrained editorial engineering.

That means:

- stronger compositional contrast;
- fewer containers;
- more proof;
- less glow;
- less permanent motion;
- more light space where the service meaning benefits;
- more explicit state/status/source labels;
- page-specific signature moments instead of site-wide gimmicks.
