# ProAI Expert — Websites & Branding R1 Concept

Status: **ONE FINAL R1 CONCEPT — OWNER REVIEW BEFORE BUILD**  
R0 decision date: 2026-08-21

# Concept name

## THE AUTHORITY FRAME — STRUCTURE MAKES TRUST LEGIBLE

## Page role

Prove that ProAI treats a website as a business-facing trust system: positioning, hierarchy, evidence, language, performance and inquiry flow working together.

The page must answer:

- why a website problem is often a business-clarity problem before it is a visual problem;
- how structure changes what the visitor understands first;
- how evidence and language influence trust;
- when custom architecture is justified and when simpler execution is enough;
- how multilingual design is handled as one operating system;
- what real project proof exists;
- what a sensible first step looks like.

## One-sentence promise

**Turn real expertise into a digital surface that makes the business easier to understand, trust and contact.**

RU should express the same meaning natively, not as a literal translation.

## Signature visual idea

The page opens around one large **editorial authority frame**.

At first, multiple pieces of information have nearly equal visual weight: offer, proof, service detail, language, next step.

On the first meaningful scroll, the composition **finds hierarchy**:

- the offer gains primary weight;
- evidence locks into a visible proof position;
- service architecture becomes secondary support;
- locale/language becomes contextual rather than competing;
- the next action resolves into one clear path.

The visual demonstrates the service thesis: **structure changes perception**.

This is not a “before / after” split, not a laptop mockup, not a design-system dashboard and not another moving rail.

## Hero

### Composition

Desktop:

- canonical Header;
- obsidian outer field;
- one large pearl/silver editorial plane occupying a meaningful share of the viewport;
- black-chrome frame/edge;
- restrained warm champagne/bronze interference only at a physical edge or rule;
- large readable H1;
- short lead explaining trust + structure + evidence;
- one primary CTA;
- one proof-oriented secondary action.

The page should contain materially **more light space than AI Systems**.

No blue/cyan premium-agency glow.

## First scroll moment

As the user enters the next chapter, the Authority Frame changes through:

- crop;
- spacing;
- type scale/weight hierarchy;
- rule position;
- proof placement.

Movement should feel like editorial composition resolving, not an animated template.

Target duration: ~900–1100ms maximum for the signature resolution, then stillness.

Reduced motion: show the final hierarchical composition immediately.

## Content chapters

### 01 — Trust starts before the first conversation

Preserve the current strategic point, but remove unsupported causal certainty.

Safe framing:

- unclear structure makes the offer harder to understand;
- weak evidence creates more uncertainty;
- inconsistent language/presentation can reduce confidence;
- a strong digital surface makes the business easier to evaluate.

Avoid claiming that design automatically increases qualified leads or conversion without evidence.

### 02 — A website is a business system

Explain the shared system:

`POSITIONING → STRUCTURE → EVIDENCE → LANGUAGE → EXPERIENCE → INQUIRY`

This is content architecture, not a horizontal animated rail.

Use an editorial arrangement with strong hierarchy and relationships.

### 03 — The Authority Frame

Demonstrate how a single service/business proposition becomes clearer as hierarchy is applied.

Possible content roles:

- primary offer;
- supporting proof;
- service architecture;
- differentiation;
- multilingual context;
- action.

The example must be framed as a design/architecture demonstration unless it uses a verified real client artifact.

### 04 — Architecture / UX / multilingual logic

This should be a major authority chapter.

Cover:

- information architecture;
- page relationships;
- responsive hierarchy;
- bilingual/locale decisions;
- when separate EN/RU wording and line breaks are needed;
- inquiry-path clarity;
- accessibility and performance as trust factors.

Do not reduce multilingual work to “we translate your site.”

### 05 — Production craft

Show implementation discipline through actual behaviors/artifacts:

- responsive states;
- typography;
- image/media handling;
- performance budget;
- accessibility;
- SEO route/canonical/hreflang preservation;
- QA.

Do not fill this chapter with technology-logo cards.

### 06 — Real work / proof

Proof hierarchy:

1. **Financial Stream** — flagship real live client proof; use verified status and current authorized evidence only.
2. **Alina Horb** — real project proof with accurate status/context.
3. **Local Repair Pro** — include only with visible demo/in-development status.
4. ProAI self-site may appear as a small craft/process note, not as evidence equal to an external client.

Real screenshots/artifacts should create the visual richness.

### 07 — How ProAI works

Evolve the current sequence into decision outputs:

`CONTEXT → ARCHITECTURE → VISUAL SYSTEM → BUILD → VERIFY`

Each stage should answer:

- what decision is made;
- what artifact/output exists;
- what is verified before proceeding.

### 08 — What the business actually needs

Provide decision guidance instead of selling maximum scope.

Possible judgments:

- template execution can be enough when positioning/structure are already solved and needs are simple;
- custom architecture is justified when trust, service complexity, evidence, multilingual structure or inquiry paths require it;
- branding work should solve recognition/coherence problems, not exist as decoration.

This chapter converts premium design from aspiration into commercial judgment.

### 09 — Next step

Primary action:

`Discuss your website`

Support intent:

“Show us the current site or describe what needs to change. We’ll identify whether the problem is positioning, structure, evidence, execution—or a smaller fix.”

RU must be native and equally low-pressure.

## Material identity

### Core

- Obsidian outer field.
- Pearl / silver editorial planes.
- Black chrome structural edges.
- Restrained warm bronze/champagne optical interference on edges/rules only.
- Shared indigo accent only for common site state/control semantics when needed.

### Prohibited

- blue-purple agency gradient;
- glowing browser window;
- laptop/device wallpaper hero;
- another giant 3D sculpture;
- glass-card portfolio wall;
- moving logo strip.

## Color logic

This page intentionally challenges the idea that ProAI premium = always dark.

Light pearl/silver space communicates:

- editorial clarity;
- confidence;
- scrutiny;
- visible craft.

Dark outer material communicates continuity with the broader ProAI brand.

Warm optical edge is a small craft signal, never the primary palette.

## Typography

Use local Inter variable.

The page should feel more editorial than AI Systems through:

- longer measured headings where needed;
- larger light fields;
- controlled contrast between display and explanatory text;
- evidence captions/source labels;
- precise line breaking.

No fashion-style giant text that sacrifices clarity.

## Motion

Motion is editorial:

- crop/mask resolve;
- rule alignment;
- hierarchy change;
- image/proof reveal;
- standard restrained chapter entries.

No constant parallax gallery.

After the Authority Frame resolves, it stays calm.

## Pointer

Fine-pointer enhancement can reveal a subtle material edge or proof caption.

No:

- mandatory hover;
- magnetic buttons;
- object-following spotlight across the entire page;
- cursor replacement.

## Mobile signature moment

Mobile hero becomes a **full-width editorial sheet** under the Header rather than a miniature desktop frame.

Opening order:

1. service/category label;
2. H1 ~38–48px depending RU/EN measure;
3. short lead;
4. primary CTA;
5. first proof/status fragment visible at or just below the first scroll.

As scrolling begins, hierarchy resolves through simple crop, spacing and rule movement. No side-by-side composition is preserved merely because desktop uses it.

## Mobile content rules

- Real proof moves earlier.
- Long architecture tables become judgment lists or labeled sequences.
- Screenshots appear near natural aspect ratio, not device frames.
- Captions/status remain attached.
- No hover.
- No tiny two-column cards.
- Decorative outer frame can collapse or disappear; the editorial sheet remains.
- Chapters should feel shorter and more decisive than desktop while preserving meaning.

## CTA

Primary: `Discuss your website`.

Secondary: a real proof route such as `View Financial Stream` or an in-page `See the work` anchor.

No booking calendar as the default conversion path.

## Difference from AI Systems

Websites & Branding is:

- lighter;
- more editorial;
- perception/hierarchy-oriented;
- evidence/media-rich;
- composed through crop, spacing and white space;
- slightly warmer in material edge behavior.

AI Systems is darker, cooler, state/sequence-oriented and operational.

## Why this can feel like a $100K bespoke page

Because the page **demonstrates its thesis through the composition itself**.

The signature behavior is not a decorative animation layered onto a service template. It shows how equal-weight information becomes a credible authority hierarchy. Real client proof supplies visual richness. Multilingual, responsive, accessibility, performance and claim governance are part of the design system rather than afterthoughts.

A generic agency can copy a black-and-white look. It cannot reproduce the page convincingly without the underlying proof system, bilingual architecture, content judgment and real implementation artifacts.

Reject the build before Owner review if it becomes:

- dark panel stack with warm accent;
- browser/laptop mockup hero;
- before/after slider gimmick;
- generic masonry portfolio;
- large 3D object;
- another homepage split interaction;
- “beautiful websites that convert” claims without evidence;
