01 · SIGNAL BEFORE SYSTEM

Work loses momentum when it loses state.

The problem is often not the absence of an AI tool. Context, ownership, routing, or the next permitted action disappears between systems and people.

01
No owner

An inquiry arrives without responsibility assigned at intake.

02
Context is detached

Customer history exists, but not where the response decision is made.

03
Route is ambiguous

Priority depends on interpretation instead of a visible operating rule.

04
Action has no boundary

The system can act, but authority is not defined explicitly.
