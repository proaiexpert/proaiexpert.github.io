# Local-Service Guardrails

Do not use without verified proof:
- fake reviews or ratings
- fake license, insured, bonded, or warranty claims
- fake completed projects
- fake prices
- guaranteed outcomes
- "free estimate" or "free consultation" unless explicitly true and approved

Safe alternatives:
- sample scenarios
- scope reviewed before scheduling
- estimate depends on photos, access, materials, location, and project details
- some work may require a licensed trade specialist or contractor

If proof is missing, document it as missing. Do not invent it.
