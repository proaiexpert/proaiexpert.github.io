# Niche Adaptation (v2)

Page-by-page adaptation playbook. Each niche section is written to be usable directly to start a new site without re-deriving structure from scratch.

## 1. Handyman / Home Repair

- Business model: small-to-mid repair jobs, hourly or scoped pricing, local service radius.
- Primary customer intent: fix something specific, fast, trustworthy, local.
- Trust signals: local presence, clear process, responsiveness, photos of finished work (if real).
- Proof required: service area, real project photos if available, response time expectations.
- Proof forbidden if unavailable: license number, insurance status, review counts, star ratings, completed-project claims.
- CTA strategy: Request Estimate as primary CTA everywhere; phone as secondary for urgent jobs.
- Homepage sections: hero (finished-result image), trust strip, service categories, scenario examples, service area, process, FAQ preview, final CTA.
- Required service pages: Services, Work Examples/Scenarios, Service Area hub + city pages, Pricing Approach, Request, FAQ, About.
- Pricing approach: factors-based explanation, no fixed prices unless confirmed.
- Service-area approach: hub page plus individual city pages, no exact-radius claims unless confirmed.
- FAQ themes: process, timing, pricing logic, service area, what is/isn't included, scope changes.
- Image direction: finished results, tools/materials in use, neighborhood context; scenario stock acceptable if labeled generically.
- Local SEO angle: city-specific landing pages, service + city combinations, natural city mentions.
- Risk/claim guardrails: no fake license/insured/warranty, no fake completed-project counts, no guaranteed pricing.
- Recommended first prototype: homepage + services + one city page + request form.

## 2. Construction / Remodeling

- Business model: larger scoped projects, consultation-driven, longer sales cycle.
- Primary customer intent: evaluate credibility and process before committing to a large spend.
- Trust signals: licensing/permits (only if real), process transparency, real project photos if permissioned.
- Proof required: licensing status (confirmed only), permit-handling process, project-type range.
- Proof forbidden if unavailable: specific completed-project counts, client testimonials, guaranteed timelines/costs.
- CTA strategy: Project Consultation request as primary CTA, framed as a scoping conversation, not a guarantee.
- Homepage sections: hero (finished-space image), process overview, project types, trust/compliance strip, FAQ preview, CTA.
- Required service pages: Project Types, Process, Service Area, About, Request/Consultation, FAQ.
- Pricing approach: range-based framing tied to project scope factors; never a flat number without confirmation.
- Service-area approach: hub page listing regions served; avoid overclaiming radius.
- FAQ themes: permitting, timeline expectations, payment structure, project management approach.
- Image direction: finished spaces, process/craftsmanship detail shots, no active-hazard or unsafe-condition imagery unless explicitly relevant and non-alarming.
- Local SEO angle: region + project-type combination pages.
- Risk/claim guardrails: no fake licensing, no fabricated safety/compliance claims, no guaranteed cost/timeline.
- Recommended first prototype: homepage + process page + request/consultation form.

## 3. Accounting / Bookkeeping / Tax Support

- Business model: recurring service (bookkeeping) plus seasonal/high-stakes service (tax).
- Primary customer intent: find someone trustworthy and competent to handle finances/taxes correctly.
- Trust signals: real credentials (e.g. CPA/EA) if claimed, software expertise, clear process, compliance awareness.
- Proof required: real credentials if claimed, software/platform expertise, service scope.
- Proof forbidden if unavailable: guaranteed refund amounts, guaranteed penalty removal, specific tax outcomes, unlicensed advice framed as legal/tax advice.
- CTA strategy: Document Review Request or Discovery Call as primary CTA.
- Homepage sections: hero (professional/organized imagery), services overview, industries served, process, compliance-boundary note, FAQ preview, CTA.
- Required service pages: Services, Industries, Process, Pricing Approach, Documents Checklist, About, Request, FAQ.
- Pricing approach: tiered/scope-based description; avoid fixed "starting at $X" unless confirmed.
- Service-area approach: remote-service framing is common; only list physical service area if a real office/region exists.
- FAQ themes: documents needed, turnaround time, confidentiality, scope of advice (bookkeeping vs tax vs advisory), compliance boundaries.
- Image direction: organized workspace, professional but approachable tone, no imagery implying guaranteed financial outcomes.
- Local SEO angle: service + region combination pages if a physical presence exists; otherwise service-type pages.
- Risk/claim guardrails: never promise specific refund amounts, penalty outcomes, or guaranteed audit protection; never frame general content as individualized tax/legal advice.
- Recommended first prototype: homepage + services + documents checklist + request form.

## 4. Dental / Implant Clinic

- Business model: appointment-based clinical service, often elective/cosmetic plus general care.
- Primary customer intent: find a trustworthy provider for a procedure, often anxious or price-sensitive.
- Trust signals: real provider credentials, real location, real insurance/payment facts, clear procedure descriptions.
- Proof required: licensed-provider facts (confirmed only), location, accepted insurance/payment options, general service descriptions.
- Proof forbidden if unavailable: outcome guarantees, before/after patient photos without explicit consent, patient testimonials without consent, medical claims beyond general/educational framing.
- CTA strategy: Request Appointment or Call Clinic as primary CTA; avoid "free consultation" unless explicitly true and approved.
- Homepage sections: hero (clinic/professional imagery, not clinical/alarming imagery), services overview, provider credentials, insurance/payment info, FAQ preview, CTA.
- Required service pages: Services/Procedures, Provider/About, Insurance and Payment, Location, Request/Appointment, FAQ.
- Pricing approach: general cost-factor education plus "confirm at consultation" framing; no guaranteed procedure prices.
- Service-area approach: single-location framing typically; list service area only if multiple confirmed locations exist.
- FAQ themes: procedure overview (general/educational only), insurance/payment, appointment process, comfort options if real.
- Image direction: clean clinical environment, professional staff (with consent), no graphic procedure imagery, no before/after without documented consent.
- Local SEO angle: procedure + location combination pages, using only confirmed real location(s).
- Risk/claim guardrails: no medical outcome guarantees, no unconsented patient imagery/testimonials, no diagnostic claims online, general educational framing only.
- Recommended first prototype: homepage + services overview + provider/location page + appointment request form.

## 5. Medical Clinic / Elective Service

- Business model: appointment-based clinical/elective service (e.g. med spa, elective procedures).
- Primary customer intent: evaluate safety, credibility, and outcome expectations for an elective procedure.
- Trust signals: real licensed-provider facts, real facility/location, clear safety/process information.
- Proof required: provider licensing (confirmed only), procedure descriptions (general/educational), facility/location facts.
- Proof forbidden if unavailable: outcome guarantees, unconsented before/after imagery or testimonials, specific result claims, unsubstantiated comparative claims.
- CTA strategy: Request Consultation as primary CTA, framed honestly (not "free" unless true).
- Homepage sections: hero (facility/professional imagery), services overview, provider credentials, safety/process overview, FAQ preview, CTA.
- Required service pages: Services/Procedures, Provider/About, Safety and Process, Request/Consultation, FAQ.
- Pricing approach: general range/factor education; no guaranteed pricing without confirmation.
- Service-area approach: single or confirmed multi-location framing only.
- FAQ themes: candidacy (general/educational only), recovery expectations (general only), safety measures, consultation process.
- Image direction: clean facility imagery, professional staff with consent, no graphic procedure imagery, no unconsented before/after.
- Local SEO angle: procedure + location pages using only confirmed locations.
- Risk/claim guardrails: no outcome guarantees, no unconsented patient content, no unsubstantiated superiority claims, general/educational framing only.
- Recommended first prototype: homepage + services overview + consultation request form.

## 6. Professional Services / Legal-Adjacent (No Legal Claims)

- Business model: consultative professional service (e.g. paralegal support, compliance consulting, business advisory) that is explicitly NOT practicing law unless the business is a licensed law firm with confirmed attorneys.
- Primary customer intent: find competent, trustworthy help navigating a complex process.
- Trust signals: real credentials/experience, clear scope-of-service boundaries, transparent process.
- Proof required: real credentials/experience if claimed, explicit scope-of-service statement, process transparency.
- Proof forbidden if unavailable: any implication of providing legal advice or an attorney-client relationship (unless a licensed attorney is confirmed), guaranteed case/outcome results, unverified credentials.
- CTA strategy: Structured Inquiry or Discovery Call as primary CTA.
- Homepage sections: hero (professional imagery), services overview, explicit scope-boundary statement, process, FAQ preview, CTA.
- Required service pages: Services, Process, Scope and Boundaries, About, Request/Inquiry, FAQ.
- Pricing approach: scope-based description; no guaranteed flat fees for outcome-dependent work unless confirmed.
- Service-area approach: jurisdiction-aware framing; only claim jurisdictions where the service is actually available/compliant.
- FAQ themes: what the service does and does not include, how it differs from hiring an attorney (if applicable), turnaround expectations, confidentiality.
- Image direction: professional, document/process-oriented imagery; avoid courtroom/gavel imagery implying legal representation unless the business is a confirmed licensed law firm.
- Local SEO angle: service + jurisdiction combination pages, using only confirmed-available jurisdictions.
- Risk/claim guardrails: never imply legal advice or representation without a confirmed licensed attorney; never guarantee case/legal outcomes; always state scope boundaries explicitly.
- Recommended first prototype: homepage + services + scope-and-boundaries page + inquiry form.
