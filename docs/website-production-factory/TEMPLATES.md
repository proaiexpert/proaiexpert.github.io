# Reusable Page Templates

## Homepage

Purpose: explain the offer, show the local-service outcome, route to service/request paths.

Core sections:
- premium hero with finished-result image
- trust/scope strip
- service categories
- scenario/work example cards
- service area
- process
- FAQ preview
- final CTA

## Services

Purpose: show what can be requested and what requires scope review.

Include:
- service category cards
- examples
- safe scope disclaimer
- CTA to request/contact

## Work Examples

Purpose: show sample scenarios without fake portfolio claims.

Use:
- scenario images
- sample context
- no "completed projects" unless verified

## Service Area Hub

Purpose: route by geography and explain local coverage.

Include:
- primary cities
- secondary cities
- map/reference image if accurate
- no exact radius unless verified

## City Page

Purpose: location-specific landing page.

Include:
- city-specific intro
- nearby service examples
- scope/permit disclaimers
- CTA

## Pricing Approach

Purpose: explain estimate logic without fake prices.

Include:
- factors affecting cost
- what helps estimate
- what is not guaranteed

## Guides

Purpose: provide helpful pre-request education without fake blog volume.

## About

Purpose: explain the site/business model without inventing history.

## Request/Contact

Purpose: collect task, location, photos, timing, and contact route.

## FAQ

Purpose: resolve objections, explain process, and define safe limitations.

## Header/Footer/Sticky CTA

Header must include core pages, phone if verified, and request CTA.
Footer must be compact, structured, and proof-safe.
Sticky CTA must not cover content.
