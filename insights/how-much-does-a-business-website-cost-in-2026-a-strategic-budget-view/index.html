A U.S. business website can cost hundreds, thousands or tens of thousands of dollars. The meaningful difference is not the number of pages: it is how much positioning, content, design, engineering, verification and ownership the project requires.

US WEBSITE PRICING · 2026

Short answer: ProAI Expert's indicative website project ranges are $700–$2,000 for Essential, $2,000–$5,000 for Business and from $5,000 for Custom & Premium. Larger bespoke website, branding and systems work may run $10,000–$20,000+ or higher. These are not fixed packages.

How much does a business website cost in the U.S. in 2026?

The difference is the work behind the visible pages: positioning, content, visual quality, mobile interaction, validation and handover. These bands describe possible ProAI Expert scope, not a universal U.S. price survey.

01 / Compact presence

Essential

$700–$2,000

A credible landing page or small site with a narrow brief and clear inquiry route.

  • Focused message and straightforward contact
  • Responsive pages within agreed scope
  • Existing content and brand assets when available
A $700 entry does not imply full custom branding, extensive writing or integrations.
02 / Core commercial fit

Business

$2,000–$5,000

The principal direction for a local service-business website: professional structure, presentation and an effective path to inquiry.

  • Service architecture and coherent visual language
  • Useful content and legible mobile interaction
  • Basic technical SEO and practical QA as scoped
Copywriting depth, unique visuals, CRM, additional languages and automation depend on the written estimate.
03 / Bespoke scope

Custom & Premium

From $5,000

For original experiences, complex content, major changes or technical integrations.

  • Distinctive architecture and designed components
  • Specialized integrations or migration if contracted
  • Expanded verification, documentation and handover
Larger bespoke branding and systems projects may cost $10,000–$20,000+ and beyond. $5,000 is not a complete-system promise.

Owner-approved ProAI Expert indicative ranges, not U.S. market averages or fixed packages. Each project is scoped individually. Final pricing depends on design, content, functionality and the work required; we provide a clear estimate after discussing your project.

What do the three ranges mean in practice?

A low-cost site can be the correct choice, while a costly site is not automatically premium. Business ($2,000–$5,000) is the core service-business segment when visitors need to evaluate several services and take a credible next step.

01 / DefineServices, target customers and the next action
02 / StructureContent, navigation and service routes
03 / VerifyMobile, forms, accessibility and SEO basics
04 / OperateAccounts, support, future changes and handover

An entry-level Business project may use approved copy, existing visual identity and ready photography. A more demanding brief may require custom editorial work, distinctive design, CRM routing, extra languages or more verification. These are quoted scope choices, not automatic inclusions.

For the separate platform decision, see builder versus custom development; for quality standards, see what makes a website premium.

What does the U.S. market evidence really measure?

Different sources measure different categories. Reviewed-project prices are not the same as one agency's stated fees, and website-builder subscriptions do not include custom development.

  • Clutch, updated September 21, 2026: says most web-design projects reviewed on its platform cost under $10,000, while listed agencies commonly charge $100–$149/hour. This is a review-based sample and agency-rate band, not a U.S.-wide median.
  • WebFX, 2026 guide: gives a broad $500–$50,000+ band for professional designs from agencies and freelancers. It is a commercial agency's cost overview, not an independent market census.
  • Huemor: describes most of its own full redesigns as $50,000–$100,000. Those larger engagements should not be presented as the typical local-service website price.
  • Platform subscriptions: Webflow lists Basic at $15/mo and Premium at $25/mo billed annually; Squarespace lists Basic at $19/mo billed annually. These are software-plan fees, not custom design or website-build quotations.

Sources checked October 24, 2026. Clutch is a review-platform sample, WebFX and Huemor are commercial providers, and Webflow and Squarespace quote software subscriptions, not custom-build projects.

ProAI Expert's indicative prices are not derived by averaging these figures. A quote must be compared on defined deliverables, responsibilities and acceptance—not a headline price.

Why do similar-size websites cost differently?

Five pages with approved content and a simple form are a different assignment from five pages requiring interviews, new copy, localization, CRM integration and legacy URL migration. Page count hides the effort and risk.

Sound production moves through strategy → architecture → content and design → implementation → QA and handover. WCAG 2.2 sets accessibility criteria, and Google Search Central documents URL migration responsibilities. Neither is a superficial checkbox.

What costs and control remain after launch?

After launch, clarify domain renewals, hosting, platform and third-party fees, optional maintenance, content changes and support. Avoid counting fees twice when bundled. Ownership needs named access to domain, DNS, analytics, content and applicable source files.

Webflow export rules explain limits for dynamic functionality. WordPress GPL terms do not themselves convey a client’s domain registrar account or third-party asset licenses.

How should a service business choose?

Essential

Choose Essential when the offer is simple, the content is ready and the visitor primarily needs one reliable contact path.

Business

Choose Business when buyers compare services, need credible proof and rely on a tested mobile inquiry journey.

Custom & Premium makes sense when distinctive design, substantial content, several languages, CRM or nonstandard workflows are real requirements. From $5,000 is only a starting point. Larger bespoke work at $10,000–$20,000+ and above needs its own brief and estimate.

A better brief can control the price

Before asking for a quotation, confirm services, audience, existing brand assets, decision owner, languages, integrations and the exact inquiry journey. Separate optional work from required deliverables. See the proposal evaluation guide and the enquiry-path analysis for deeper checks.

Ask for a scoped estimate

Choose the smallest scope that reliably serves customers, then request a written estimate. Essential can be the right first step; Business is a practical starting point for established local service firms; Custom is justified by real requirements, not status.
