Human handoff guardrails for AI agents

March 20, 2026 • Guardrails

Every production agent needs explicit boundaries. Define when to stop, when to ask, and when to transfer to a human. This protects trust and conversion.

  • Stop on missing critical data.
  • Escalate on legal/financial uncertainty.
  • Require confirmation before external actions.
  • Keep full logs for every decision branch.
