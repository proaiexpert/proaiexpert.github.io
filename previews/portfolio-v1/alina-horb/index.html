01 · The challenge

A sensitive service cannot rely on noise, pressure or borrowed trust.

The website had to help a person understand the specialist, the working format and the next step — without fake reviews, clinical coldness, generic wellness imagery or promises that the project could not substantiate.

01

Personal credibility

Build confidence around a real professional and verifiable facts, without inventing a clinic or corporate persona.

02

Two language audiences

Create natural Ukrainian and Russian experiences with page-for-page context preserved across the entire site.

03

A careful first step

Make consultation terms and boundaries clear before asking a visitor to send any personal information.

04

Depth without overload

Combine substantial content, professional boundaries and technical detail while keeping the experience calm.

02 · Visual direction

Premium Editorial Sanctuary.

An editorial composition replaces the expected therapist template. A restrained palette, serif hierarchy, numbered rail and arched portrait create an individual identity while preserving space and readability.

04 · Consultation journey

The path to contact starts with clarity, not a form.

Visitors can first understand the format, duration, price, languages, boundaries and frequently asked questions. Only then does the site invite a short first-contact message.

01

Understand

Format, duration, cost, languages and boundaries are visible before contact.

02

Ask

FAQ reduces uncertainty around the first conversation and organizational details.

03

Send a short request

Only name, contact, a brief message and consent are required; organizational fields remain optional.

04

Receive confirmation

The form is replaced by a localized success panel only after a successful response.

05

Keep a fallback

If the form service is temporarily unavailable, an email route remains available.

Low-friction intake

Compact by design.

Optional fields do not become a barrier, blank values do not clutter the notification, and the interface states remain localized.
