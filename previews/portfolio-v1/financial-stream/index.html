01 · Business context

Trust had to begin before the first conversation.

Financial Stream needed more than a polished brochure. The website had to explain a broad professional-service scope, support two language audiences, reduce uncertainty before contact, and create a practical path from first visit to a useful request.

02 · Connected system

Not one feature. One coordinated client-entry system.

The case is structured as a connected map. As each layer becomes active, the visual focus changes from presentation to service clarity, intake, content, and human-reviewed operational support.

01

Bilingual architecture

English and Russian are treated as coordinated editorial systems with paired routes, navigation logic, canonical and hreflang foundations — not as an automatic translation layer.

02

Service structure — including Payroll

Business setup, bookkeeping, Payroll, reporting, tax preparation, document review and consulting are organized so visitors can identify the right direction before reaching out.

03

Structured intake first

A deeper request path collects context, period, documents, deadlines and current setup. A shorter website form remains available for low-friction questions.

04

Calendar after context

Booking is positioned as a secondary step after the business has enough information to make a call useful, rather than forcing every visitor directly into a meeting.

05

Content, local relevance and search depth

Service pages, bilingual articles, materials, FAQ, legal pages and local context turn the site into a deeper long-term digital asset rather than a thin homepage.

06

AI-assisted support with human control

Chatbase supports first-line navigation. A Gmail + Make + OpenAI workflow prepares drafts with language routing and human review. Partial Twilio work remains labelled separately rather than presented as fully autonomous production automation.
